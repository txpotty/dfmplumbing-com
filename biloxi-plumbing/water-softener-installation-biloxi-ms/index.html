    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Biloxi

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Biloxi

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Biloxi, MS

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. You will taste it. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Biloxi, MS

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Filters add cost. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Biloxi

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Biloxi, MS

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Water Softener Replacement in Biloxi, MS

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.
