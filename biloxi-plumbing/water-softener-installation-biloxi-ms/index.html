    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Biloxi

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. DFM Plumbing expertly installs various models of salt-based softeners, making sure of proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Biloxi home.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Biloxi

    Accurate testing is key for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Biloxi, MS

    Municipal water often contains chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter paired with a softener removes those chemicals and protects resin. We install combination systems with filter and softener tanks or dual-stage units. This setup improves both water quality and feel. Many homeowners notice a big difference in taste.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Biloxi, MS

    Softener installation costs depend on system type, capacity and whether you need a loop. Installing on an existing loop is faster and costs less than adding a new loop. Salt-free systems and combination filters vary in price. We give you a written quote that includes equipment, labor and setup. We also explain long-term salt and maintenance costs.
