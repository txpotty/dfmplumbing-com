    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. The valve goes in next. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.

    Shower Pan Installation in Biloxi

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. We level prefab pans carefully. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Biloxi

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.
