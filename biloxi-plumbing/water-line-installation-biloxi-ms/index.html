    Expansive clay soils are common in Mississippi, swelling with rain and shrinking in dry spells, which puts stress on foundations and buried pipes. Our installation techniques account for local soil conditions in Biloxi to reduce future issues.

    For most homes, PEX and copper are usually the best materials. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and handles minor ground shifts well.     PEX is flexible, less expensive, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and has fewer fittings, which means fewer potential leak points.
