    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Biloxi, MS

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Biloxi, MS

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Some qualify for tax credits. We help you decide if one fits.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Biloxi

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Biloxi

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Biloxi, MS

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Leak checks follow. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Biloxi, MS

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
