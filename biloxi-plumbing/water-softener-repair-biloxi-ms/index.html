    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Biloxi

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. Urgent calls like that get priority. The crew in Biloxi puts the unit in bypass first, then finds out whether the valve, the timer or the drain line caused it. Most of the time it is a stuck piston or a failed motor that can be replaced on the same visit.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Biloxi

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Biloxi

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. We test each part. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Biloxi, MS

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. Then it gets a pressure test.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Biloxi, MS

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Biloxi, MS

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. DFM Plumbing repairs these units in Biloxi, MS. Media lasts a few years.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Biloxi

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Biloxi, MS

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.
