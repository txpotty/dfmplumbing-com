    Main Line Drain Repair in Biloxi

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. The lowest fixture backs up first.     Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Biloxi, MS

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Biloxi, MS

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. The repair gets a flow test.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Biloxi

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Find it and fix it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. We repair the part that catches debris.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Biloxi, MS

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Repairs meet local plumbing code.

Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.
