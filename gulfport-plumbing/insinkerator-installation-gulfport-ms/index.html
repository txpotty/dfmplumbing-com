    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Gulfport

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. The hose clamps onto the inlet and runs through an air gap or a high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from siphoning back into the tub. We test both appliances at once.

    Upfront Disposal Installation Pricing in Gulfport, MS

    Price depends mostly on two things: the unit you pick and how much needs to change under the sink. A like-for-like swap is the low end. A first-time install that needs a new flange and a new outlet costs more, because that is real labor and sometimes an electrician's visit too. You get the number before we start.

    Badger Series Installation in Gulfport, MS

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. All of them use the same quick-lock mount. Because of that, a Badger-for-Badger swap can take under an hour, and the flange often stays if it is still clean. Badgers ship without a cord, so we bring the cord kit.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Gulfport, MS

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot. What they notice later is fewer clogs downstream, because the multi-stage grind breaks fibrous scraps into much smaller pieces before they reach the trap and the branch line. We check for vibration with the unit running.

    Septic-Friendly Disposal Installs in Gulfport, MS

    Homes on septic can run a disposal, but food solids add load to the tank. Regular pumping and inspection keep a septic system working, and slow drains in a rural home often point to the tank first. InSinkErator's Septic Assist feeds an enzyme solution into the chamber during each run to start breaking down waste before the tank. We mount the cartridge and show you how to swap it. Scraping plates into the trash still helps.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in Gulfport, MS

    Old disposals are heavy and awkward to get rid of. They leave on our truck. The old cord, any replaced mount ring and the rusty slip nuts go too, so you are not stuck with a bag of parts on trash day. One visit handles most of these.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Gulfport

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.

    New Construction Disposal Installs in Gulfport
