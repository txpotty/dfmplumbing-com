    How We Replace Polybutylene in Gulfport, MS

    Polybutylene to PEX Transitions in Gulfport, MS

    Sometimes the yard service line coming into the house is not polybutylene, or it was replaced already. In that case, we connect the new PEX system to the existing service at the main shutoff with an approved transition fitting, which avoids digging up the yard. We check the service line first.     Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Gulfport

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Gulfport

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. It also tolerates freezing better. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Gulfport

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. We run new lines from the main shutoff to every sink, toilet, tub, washer, ice maker and water heater, add new quarter-turn stops and test the whole system before patching.

    Partial Polybutylene Replacement in Gulfport, MS

    Full replacement is the right long-term fix, but it is not always possible right away. Sometimes it has to wait. Replacing the riskiest parts first, near the heater and in the attic, lowers the odds of a burst while you plan the rest. We will be honest about what is left.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Gulfport, MS

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance, and most homes are completed in two to four working days with water back on each night.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Gulfport, MS

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. These buried lines can split under the yard, raising the water bill and soaking the soil. We call 811 first. After locating, we trench, lay new PEX or copper and tie it into a new main shutoff at the house.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Gulfport, MS

    Some homeowners in Gulfport, MS want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.
