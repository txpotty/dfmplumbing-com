    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Gulfport, MS

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port.     Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Gulfport, MS

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open.     Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Gulfport

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Gulfport

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes.
