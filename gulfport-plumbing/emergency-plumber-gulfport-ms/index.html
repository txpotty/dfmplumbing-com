    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Gulfport, MS

    Slab leaks can cause serious structural damage and high water bills. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can crack drain lines and supply pipes. Our emergency team uses acoustic leak detection and thermal imaging to pinpoint the exact spot of the leak under your Gulfport home's foundation.
