    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Gulfport

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Gulfport, MS

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Gulfport

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Gulfport, MS

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Leak checks follow. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Gulfport, MS

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. You get more spots to put it.
