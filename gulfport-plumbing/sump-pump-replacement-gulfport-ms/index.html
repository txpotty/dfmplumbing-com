    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Gulfport, MS

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most.     High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Gulfport

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle significant water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Gulfport

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Gulfport home through extended power interruptions. You get peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Gulfport

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Gulfport, MS

    For maximum protection, many homeowners in Gulfport choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. DFM Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are well matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Gulfport

    Wayne sump pumps offer a range of efficient models, from entry-level to professional-grade. If your Wayne sump pump is failing, DFM Plumbing provides prompt Wayne sump pump replacement in Gulfport. We make sure the new pump is correctly installed with proper discharge and check valve components, restoring your home's flood protection quickly and efficiently.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Gulfport

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.
