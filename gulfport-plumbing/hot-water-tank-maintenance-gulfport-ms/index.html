    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Gulfport

    In mineral-heavy water a magnesium rod may last three to five years. We measure it at every visit so you replace it before the tank lining begins to rust.

    Iron can leave orange stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself.     Routine hot water tank maintenance, such as flushing and anode rod replacement, usually does not require a permit in Gulfport, MS. However, if major repairs or a full replacement are needed, a permit may be required to ensure compliance with local building codes.

    Many homeowners can do a basic drain with a garden hose. The risk is a plastic drain valve that breaks or will not reseal, and DIY flushes rarely include an anode check or relief valve test.
