    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Gulfport

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Older homes in Mississippi often have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains, both of which corrode and restrict flow with age. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. We price each one for you.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Gulfport, MS

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.
