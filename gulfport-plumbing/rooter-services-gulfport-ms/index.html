    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Gulfport

    Plan on 1 to 3 hours for a single clogged drain. Heavy main line root cutting or a long jetting run can take 3 to 6 hours. You get a time estimate first. After looking at the cleanout and hearing which drains are slow, the tech can narrow the estimate.

    Gurgling toilets, sewage odors and slow drains in several fixtures are the typical signs. Water backing up into a tub when you flush is another clue. Take that one seriously.     Clay or older cast iron lines with trees nearby usually need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera look can set the timing. How quickly roots return after the last cut tells us if yearly or every-other-year service fits your line.

    Clearing a clog in Gulfport does not need a permit. Repairs are another matter. If the camera shows broken pipe that needs a dig, a new cleanout or a sewer replacement, a plumbing permit is usually required. We handle the permit and the inspection.
