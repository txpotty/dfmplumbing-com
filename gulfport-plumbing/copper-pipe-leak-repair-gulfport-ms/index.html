    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Gulfport

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting. One usually means more are coming.

    Under Slab Copper Leak Repair in Gulfport, MS

    Copper lines under a slab can wear through where they rub concrete or gravel. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. Both options get a price.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Gulfport, MS

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes.
