    CIPP (cured-in-place pipe) lining involves inserting a new liner inside the existing pipe to create a pipe within a pipe. Pipe bursting, on the other hand, involves breaking apart the old pipe while pulling a new pipe into its place, resulting in a completely new pipe.

    Most common pipe materials, including cast iron, clay, PVC, and concrete, can be repaired using trenchless methods. However, the condition of the existing pipe is a factor. If a pipe is completely collapsed or severely misaligned, pipe bursting might be a more suitable trenchless option than lining. Our inspection will determine the best approach.

These repairs are resistant to corrosion, root intrusion, and chemical damage.
