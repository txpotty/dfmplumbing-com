    Jet Pump System Repair in Gulfport, MS

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. DFM Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Gulfport operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Gulfport

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Gulfport, MS

    Control and pressure switch repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire. If your pump is older and already needs pulling, replacing it at the same time can save a second pull later. We lay out both options with honest numbers.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Gulfport

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Gulfport.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Gulfport

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a stubborn problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts thorough diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Gulfport home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.
