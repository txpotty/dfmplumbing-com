    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Gulfport

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, oddly green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, targeted hole.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leak Detection in Gulfport

    Leaks inside walls and ceilings often show up as stains, bubbling paint, soft drywall or a musty smell. We use thermal cameras to find temperature differences caused by water, then confirm with moisture meters. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line. Pinpointing the source lets us cut a small, neat access opening.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Gulfport

    Summer heat and wind can evaporate a lot of pool water, so it can be hard to tell normal loss from a leak. A bucket test compares evaporation to actual water loss. We then pressure test plumbing lines and use dye testing around lights, skimmers and fittings. Finding the leak safeguards the pool shell and the soil around it.

    Irrigation and Yard Leak Detection in Gulfport, MS

    Drip and sprinkler systems are a common source of hidden water loss in many yards. A cracked lateral or a stuck valve can waste thousands of gallons before anyone notices. We test the irrigation system separately from the house and trace leaks along buried lines. Many water providers recommend checking irrigation first when bills jump in summer.
