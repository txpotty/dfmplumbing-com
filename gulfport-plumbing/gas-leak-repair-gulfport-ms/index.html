    What Our Gas Leak Repair Visits Cover in Gulfport, MS

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Gulfport

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. If your range or water heater still has one, it should come out. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the burner is lit.

    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Gulfport, MS

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising. The first-stage regulator at the tank and the second-stage regulator at the house both get checked. Low spots get sniffed first. If the supplier owns the tank, they handle the tank side and we handle the piping from there to the appliances. Rural properties with underground tanks get a line test too.

    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Gulfport, MS

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company. It is also the stretch that takes the most weather, sitting in the sun or the soil. Risers rust at grade. We replace the riser or the buried run, pressure test it and leave the meter connection ready for the utility to open the meter. Expect a half day for a typical riser swap.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Gulfport, MS

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Every time somebody slides the stove out to mop, the connector takes another bend. Valves weep at the stem. The fix is usually a new coated connector and a new ball valve, plus a drip leg if the appliance lacks one. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is lit again.

    Gas Line Repair and Replacement in Gulfport, MS

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. The joint gets broken, cleaned and remade with new pipe dope rated for gas. Cracked fittings get replaced. Badly rusted sections, often near a crawlspace vent, are cut back to clean pipe and replaced. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a gauge test.

    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Gulfport

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator. We schedule the work around your service hours when the leak allows it. Fryers on quick-disconnect hoses fail often. Each hose and manifold joint gets tested, and we tag what we isolated so your staff knows what is off. New pipe still needs the commercial sign-off.

    Permitting and Code Compliance in Gulfport, MS

    Most jurisdictions want a permit for new gas pipe or a moved line, though a straight connector swap often does not need one. We tell you up front which way your job falls. The fee goes on the estimate. The work follows Mississippi adopted fuel gas code and any local amendments. When the inspector signs off, you get the paperwork, which matters later when the house is sold and a buyer's inspector asks about the gas work.

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Gulfport

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it. The tag usually says what they found, so we start there. Then we test everything. Once the repair is done, we perform the required test and send the documentation to the utility or the inspector. Relights are scheduled with the utility, usually within a day.
