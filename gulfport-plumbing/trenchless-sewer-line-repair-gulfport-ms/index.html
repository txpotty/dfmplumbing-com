    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Gulfport, MS

    Sewer lines in Gulfport, MS can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Gulfport, MS

    While the upfront cost of trenchless sewer repair in Gulfport, MS can sometimes be comparable to or slightly higher than traditional methods, the overall savings are often substantial. This is because trenchless repairs eliminate the need for extensive excavation, backfilling and landscape restoration. Homeowners save money on repairing driveways, patios and gardens, making it a more sensible choice in the long run for many.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Gulfport, MS

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral often runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. We price both options so you can decide.

    Sectional Point Repair in Gulfport

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Gulfport, MS

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Pipe Bursting in Gulfport, MS

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.
