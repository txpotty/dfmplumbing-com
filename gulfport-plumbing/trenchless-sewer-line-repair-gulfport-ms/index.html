    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Gulfport

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line at high pressure to strip roots, grease and loose scale, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Sectional Point Repairs in Gulfport

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. We push it in on a packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. One crack, one patch. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Gulfport, MS

    Restaurants feel a sewer failure fast, because grease and a dish machine put heavy load on a 4- or 6-inch building drain all day. Lining can be scheduled overnight or on a day you are closed. A trench through a parking lot means lost spaces and a blocked entrance for a week or more. Liners skip that.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in Gulfport, MS

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once in, they fill the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Gulfport

    We record the full camera run on every job, from the cleanout out to the city main or the septic tank. You get the file. On the footage you can see what we see, roots at a joint at 22 feet and an offset at 38 feet where the pipe dropped. Those footage marks tell us where to line and where a point repair will do.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Gulfport

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Repair in Gulfport

    Sewage coming up through a floor drain is an emergency, but the first fix is usually a cable or a jetter to get the line flowing again. The permanent trenchless repair follows once the pipe is open and on camera. DFM Plumbing can often schedule the liner within the same week, once the pipe has been cleaned and measured. In the meantime the house drains normally.

    Compare total cost, not just the plumbing line on the invoice. A dig job looks cheaper on paper, until you add saw-cutting the driveway and pouring new concrete over a trench that may run 50 feet. Trenchless avoids most of that. Sod and sprinkler repair add up too.
