    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer in Gulfport

    Annual residential testing runs $75 to $150 per assembly. Repairs with a rebuild kit typically run $150 to $450. Residential installs run about $300 to $1,200, while a commercial RPZ is often $1,500 to $5,000 installed with its drain and enclosure.

    A brass assembly often lasts 10 to 20 years, sometimes longer. The rubber parts inside wear out much sooner, which is why rebuild kits exist and why most failed tests are fixed without replacing the body. Cold snaps are hard on them.

    Most water providers, including those around Gulfport, require a test annually. Some also require a test right after installation, after a repair, or any time the assembly is moved to a new spot. Your notice lists the date.

    New installations and replacements often require a plumbing permit and an initial test. Each city and provider sets its own rules. We pull permits and handle the initial test, so the paperwork is done before the assembly goes into regular service.

    Water dripping or running from the relief valve on an RPZ is the clearest sign. A pressure drop, a split bonnet after cold weather or water with an odd taste can also mean trouble. Have it checked promptly.

    Look for a steady leak from the device, a sudden drop in water pressure or discolored water at the tap. On an RPZ, water spitting from the relief port every time pressure changes is a sign a check is leaking back. Get it tested soon.

    Water providers may send follow-up notices, charge fees or eventually suspend service. Scheduling early avoids these problems. We can often fit urgent tests in on short notice, even when the deadline is only a few days away.

    Once a year is the usual requirement. Some providers require it after installation, repair or relocation as well. The deadline is on your notice, and we can usually get there within days.
