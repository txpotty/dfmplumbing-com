    Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance in Gulfport

    Pumping frequency comes from two numbers: how big the trap is and how fast your kitchen fills it. The first few visits, we measure fill rate with a sludge judge. After that the date goes on our calendar and the truck arrives without a reminder call from you. Schedules shift if your menu does.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Gulfport, MS

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two. The service covers grease and solids removal, a baffle scrape, a look at the inlet flow control fitting, and a new lid gasket if the old one is flattened. A bad gasket is why most of these smell.

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Gulfport

    Indoor traps usually sit under the three-compartment sink or in a recessed pit near the dish station. Room to work is tight. Mats go down first, the contents get pumped or scooped into sealed containers, and the floor gets wiped so prep can start right after. We also check the gasket and the flow control.

    Grease Trap Inspection and Repair in Gulfport

    An empty trap is the only time anyone can see what is wrong with it. We look for cracked baffles and missing outlet tees. A missing outlet tee lets the floating grease cap pour straight into the sewer, and it is one of the most common failures we find on older concrete interceptors. We can often fix small problems on the spot.

    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Gulfport

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster. Pumping and hauling are both on us. Everything goes into a sealed tank on the truck, and each load is logged with the gallons and where it went. No barrels left behind.

    Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Gulfport

    A trap that stinks between pump-outs usually has a flattened lid gasket or a vent problem. Both get checked every visit. Gaskets are cheap to swap, but a broken vent can push sewer gas into the kitchen and slow the drain too, so it is worth chasing. Odors are often fixed the same day.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Gulfport, MS

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. Pumping alone leaves that crust in place. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. Plan on about an extra hour.
