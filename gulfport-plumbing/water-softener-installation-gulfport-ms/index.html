    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Gulfport, MS

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. DFM Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Gulfport

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Gulfport

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Gulfport, MS

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Carbon tanks cost extra. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Gulfport

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Gulfport, MS

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Dishwashers and boilers last longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Gulfport, MS

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Startup matters. Every step gets done.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Gulfport

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.
