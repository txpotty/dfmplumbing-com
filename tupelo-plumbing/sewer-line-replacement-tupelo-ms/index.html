    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Tupelo, MS

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Tupelo, MS while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Replacement in Tupelo

    Sewer line replacement in Tupelo commonly ranges from about $4,000 to $15,000 for a typical residential lateral, and can run higher for long, deep or hard-to-reach lines. Trenchless work is often priced around $80 to $250 per foot depending on conditions.

    Trenchless methods disturb less of your yard and are great under driveways and trees. Open-trench can be better for fixing grade problems, and may cost less in an open yard.

    With traditional trenching, some disruption to your yard is unavoidable, but we work to limit it and restore the area as well as possible. If trenchless methods are suitable, yard damage is greatly reduced, often limited to small access points.

    The timeline for sewer line replacement varies on the method and scope of work. A trenchless replacement can often be completed in 1 to 3 days, while a traditional excavation might take 3 to 5 days, sometimes longer for large projects or tight access. We will provide an estimated timeline during your consultation.

    Modern sewer lines made from materials like PVC or HDPE, when properly installed, can last 50 to 100 years or even longer. This is a significant improvement over older materials like cast iron or clay, which typically have a shorter lifespan.

    Sewer line replacement is a demanding job that requires specialized equipment, permits, and expertise in excavation, pipe fitting, and local plumbing codes. It is not a good DIY project and should always be handled by licensed, experienced professionals for safety and compliance.

    We primarily use high-quality PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or HDPE (high-density polyethylene) pipe for sewer line replacements. These materials are durable, corrosion-resistant, flexible, and long-lived, making them well suited for the soil conditions in Mississippi.
