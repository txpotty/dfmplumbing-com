    Sump Pump Maintenance in Tupelo, MS: What We Check

    Zoeller Sump Pump Service in Tupelo

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-Ups in Tupelo

    Once a year is the minimum. Each yearly visit covers the motor, the impeller, the float and the discharge line. Then we fill the pit and time how long the pump takes to empty it, because a slow drawdown is the earliest sign of a worn impeller or a partly blocked line.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Tupelo, MS

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Tupelo

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. Our plumber arrives with spare floats, check valves and a replacement pump in the van. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Tupelo, MS

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Plenty of crawlspaces and deep window wells use a small pump to clear storm runoff. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Exposed pipes in crawlspaces, outdoor spigots and well equipment are the most vulnerable during a rare deep cold snap.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Tupelo

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller. Our plumber pumps the basin down, rinses the screen and inspects the liner for cracks that let dirt seep in through the walls. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Wayne Sump Pump Service in Tupelo, MS

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Tupelo

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.
