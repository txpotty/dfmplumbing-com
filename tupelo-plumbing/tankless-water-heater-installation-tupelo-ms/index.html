    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Tupelo, MS

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes. Over time, lower standby energy losses and a longer expected lifespan make up for part of that cost. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper descaling, compared with about 8 to 12 years for many tanks in hard water areas. We give you a side-by-side quote so you can decide what fits your budget.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Tupelo, MS

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Iron can leave orange stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
