    Choosing a Backup Battery in Tupelo

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Tupelo

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Duplex setups with alternating pumps give you a spare if one quits. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Tupelo

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Tupelo

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most.
