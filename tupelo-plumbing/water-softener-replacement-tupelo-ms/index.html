    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Tupelo, MS

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Tupelo

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Tupelo

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    Most residential water softener replacements can be finished within 3 to 6 hours, assuming no major plumbing changes are required. Our experienced plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption, restoring soft water to your home on the same day.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take a bit longer.

    Homes around Tupelo on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a filter. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, reduced efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is essential for combating these issues in Tupelo homes.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can investigate other causes.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear out sooner with very hard or chlorinated water.

    New water softeners usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. DFM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.
