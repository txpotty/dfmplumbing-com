    Copper to PEX Conversions in Tupelo

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Iron can leave orange stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Tupelo, MS

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Tupelo, MS

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Tupelo

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Tupelo can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
