    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Tupelo

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Tupelo, MS home.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Tupelo

    PEX and CPVC do not carry sound well. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, even through grout lines and small cracks. It catches leaks the microphone cannot in Tupelo homes.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Tupelo

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Tupelo

    The hot side fails more than the cold. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Tupelo, MS

    One leak on healthy pipe is a good spot-repair candidate. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, replace the bad section and hold a pressure test. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. Accurate marks keep that hole small.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Tupelo, MS

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.
