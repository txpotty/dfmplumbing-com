    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases.     Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Clinton

    The cost for tankless water heater replacement in Clinton, MS typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's brand, capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed changes to gas lines, electrical or venting. This estimate includes both the new unit and professional installation. We always provide a detailed quote upfront after assessing your needs.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a new tankless water heater typically lasts 20 to 25 years. This is much longer than traditional tank water heaters, which usually last 10 to 15 years. Regular flushing and descaling can help extend the life of your tankless unit, especially in hard water areas.

    New tankless water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the heat exchanger, usually 10 to 15 years, and on parts, usually 3 to 5 years. DFM Plumbing also provides a warranty on our installation workmanship. We will go over all warranty details with you before your replacement so you understand your coverage.

    To prepare for tankless water heater replacement, make sure the area around your existing unit is clear and accessible for our plumbers. If possible, clear a path to the installation site and keep pets or children away from the work area. We will handle draining the old unit and all other technical preparations.

    DFM Plumbing installs and replaces all major reputable brands of tankless water heaters. Our preferred brands include Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem, as they are known for their reliability, energy efficiency and long-term performance. We can also source and install other brands based on your preference or home requirements.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement usually takes between 4 and 8 hours. This timeframe can vary based on whether we are replacing an existing tankless unit with a similar model or upgrading from a traditional tank water heater. A tank conversion may require more extensive changes to gas lines, electrical or venting.

    Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include frequent error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water. If your unit is over 15 to 20 years old and repairs are becoming frequent and costly, replacement is often the more economical choice.
