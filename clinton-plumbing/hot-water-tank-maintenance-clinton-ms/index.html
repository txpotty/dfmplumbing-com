    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Clinton, MS

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is essential to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and maintaining system integrity.

    Commercial and Rental Tank Maintenance in Clinton

    Property managers, restaurants and small offices often run several tanks that work much harder than a home unit. We set up scheduled maintenance for landlords and businesses, flushing each tank and logging the anode condition and valve test date. Records like these help with insurance and with planning replacements before a tank fails during business hours. We service 50 to 100 gallon commercial models as well as residential tanks in rental units.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Clinton, MS

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Iron can leave rusty stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Clinton, MS, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Clinton

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure spike every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Commercial Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Clinton, MS

    Businesses in Clinton, MS rely closely on consistent hot water for their operations. Our commercial hot water tank maintenance services are tailored to the higher demands of commercial units.     Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Clinton

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Clinton, MS

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Clinton, MS

    A annual flush costs a small fraction of a new tank, and a new installed tank often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more. We give you an honest read on whether maintenance is still worth it. If a tank is past 10 to 12 years old, shows rust at the base or leaks from the seams, we will tell you replacement is the smarter spend. When the tank still has life, maintenance keeps it running well for years.
