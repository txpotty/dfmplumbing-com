    Jetting a Sewer Line in Clinton, MS

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Clinton

    The main carries everything out of the house. A blockage there stops every drain. Our machines push a cutting head through the full length of the lateral until it breaks through and flow returns, which is common work in the older homes around Clinton.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Clinton, MS

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Some homes from the 1980s and 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which is known for sudden leaks and is usually replaced. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Clinton, MS

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Clinton, MS

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Clinton, MS

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Clinton

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.
