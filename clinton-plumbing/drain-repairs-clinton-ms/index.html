    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Clinton, MS

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there.     Drain Vent Repair in Clinton, MS

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. Air cannot get in behind the water. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, which is why we inspect the roof opening and the vent piping inside the walls. Many vent fixes take under an hour.

    Storm Drain Repair in Clinton, MS

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Hurricanes, tropical storms and heavy rain bring flooding risk to Mississippi, especially in low-lying and coastal areas.     Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom.
