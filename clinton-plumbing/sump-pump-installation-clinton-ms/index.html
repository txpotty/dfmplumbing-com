    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Clinton, MS

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet.     Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Clinton, MS

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Clinton

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Clinton

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Clinton, MS

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Clinton, MS

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Clinton

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Clinton, MS

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.
