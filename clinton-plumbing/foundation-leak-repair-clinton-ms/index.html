    Foundation Leak Detection in Clinton

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Clinton

    Newer homes often feature PEX or PVC plumbing, which, while durable, can still develop foundation leaks due to ground movement or improper installation. DFM Plumbing has the tools and expertise to repair leaks in these plastic piping systems. We use correct fusion and solvent welding techniques to ensure strong, leak-proof connections, restoring the integrity of your plumbing system beneath your Clinton foundation.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Clinton, MS

    Many older homes in Clinton, MS have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Iron can leave orange stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they need specialized repair. Our technicians repair or replace sections of damaged copper piping, ensuring a durable seal that holds up to pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Clinton, MS

    In some cases, the most effective way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. This method lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe directly without breaking through your home's interior flooring. DFM Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, minimizing disruption to your Clinton, MS home and delivering a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Clinton, MS

    Direct repairs are often the lowest upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and may need flooring repairs. Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in the abandoned line. A full repipe is the most comprehensive option for homes with multiple leaks or aging pipe. We help you compare total costs, including flooring and drywall repairs.
