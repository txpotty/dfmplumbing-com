    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Clinton

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free.     Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Clinton

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. The run from the sinks to the trap is where grease cools and builds up.     Baffle Repair and Replacement in Clinton

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Both require a full pump-out and a dry tank.

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Clinton, MS

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined.
