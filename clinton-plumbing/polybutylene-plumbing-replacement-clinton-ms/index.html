    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Clinton, MS

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings.     Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Clinton

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. We run new lines from the main shutoff to every sink, toilet, tub, washer, ice maker and water heater, add new quarter-turn stops and test the whole system before patching.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Clinton

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. If the meter's leak dial moves with everything off, we track the leak down with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging instead of guessing. Once we find it, a temporary fix buys time to schedule the repipe.
