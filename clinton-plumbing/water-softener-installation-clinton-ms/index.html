    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Clinton

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Clinton, MS

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Dishwashers and boilers last longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Clinton, MS

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Clinton, MS

    Building a home in Clinton, MS? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. We work with builders. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Clinton

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Clinton

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Clinton, MS

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. DFM Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Softener Loop Installation in Clinton

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.
