    Main Water Line Replacement in Clinton

    Over time, main water lines can corrode, crack or leak because of shifting soil, tree root intrusion or age.     PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Clinton, MS

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it with care on every job site.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Clinton, MS

    Copper has been a proven material for water lines for decades, known for its longevity, antimicrobial properties and resistance to high temperatures and pressure. DFM Plumbing provides professional copper water line installation in Clinton, MS, for homeowners who prefer its long history. While often more expensive upfront, copper offers superior reliability and can add value to your property. Our skilled plumbers deliver precise solder joints and secure connections.

    Water Line Installation Cost Comparison in Clinton, MS

    Cost depends largely on run length, soil conditions and pipe material. A short PEX run through soft soil is the most affordable option, while long copper runs through rocky ground cost more in both labor and materials. Trenchless methods cost more per foot but can save thousands in concrete and landscape repair. We provide a written quote that breaks out trenching, pipe, fittings, permits and restoration.
