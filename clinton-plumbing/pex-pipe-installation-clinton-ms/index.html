    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Clinton

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in often run $800 to $2,500.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage contributes to its long-term reliability, making it a smart investment for your Clinton home.

    We mainly use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in Mississippi plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has fewer joints. Copper is very durable but can develop pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    DFM Plumbing stands behind our PEX pipe installations with a comprehensive warranty on both labor and materials. Specific warranty details will be provided with your estimate, but you can be sure your new PEX system is installed correctly and will perform reliably for years to come in your Clinton home.

    We install Uponor PEX-A along with Viega, Zurn and SharkBite PEX-B systems. We recommend the system that suits your project and budget.

    A full home PEX repipe for an average-sized home in Clinton usually takes 3 to 5 days. Smaller projects or single-line replacements can often be completed in a single day. We aim to minimize disruption and communicate timelines clearly.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is highly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are crucial for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.
