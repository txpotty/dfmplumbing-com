    Top-mount Sink Installation in Clinton

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Mississippi homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still important to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Clinton install top-mount sinks, applying durable sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.

    Under-mount Sink Installation in Clinton, MS

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. DFM Plumbing excels in under-mount sink installation, ensuring a strong bond and a perfectly sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We ensure the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for optimal drainage.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Clinton

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining well. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.

    Composite sinks, made from materials like granite or quartz composites, offer excellent durability and a wide range of colors to match your kitchen decor. Our plumbers in Clinton are experienced with composite sink installation, using the right tools and techniques for secure mounting and sealing.
