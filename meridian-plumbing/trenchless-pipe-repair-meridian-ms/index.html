    Pipe Bursting in Meridian, MS

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting requires an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Meridian

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Meridian

    Not every pipe needs to be lined end to end. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, we can install a short sectional liner. This costs less than a full-length liner and is completed quickly. We confirm the rest of the line is sound before recommending a spot liner.
