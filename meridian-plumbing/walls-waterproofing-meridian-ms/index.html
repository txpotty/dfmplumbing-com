    Basement Wall Sealing in Meridian, MS

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. Long, hot and humid summers are the main stress on plumbing in Mississippi, and humidity keeps crawlspaces damp for much of the year. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your Meridian, MS basement.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Meridian

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Drainage System Integration in Meridian, MS

    Effective walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Meridian, MS properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Meridian

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Meridian, MS

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Meridian

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Meridian, MS

    Sump pumps are a critical part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed correctly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, typically managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies significantly.     Key signs you need walls waterproofing include damp or wet basement walls, visible mold or mildew growth, a musty odor in your basement or crawl space, efflorescence on concrete walls, cracks in the foundation, or standing water after heavy rains.
