    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Meridian

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Water Heater Inspections in Meridian

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Meridian, MS

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Meridian

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.
