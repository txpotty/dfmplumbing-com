    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Meridian, MS

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We focus on replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Meridian

    A leaky toilet can waste hundreds of gallons of water each year, increasing your utility bills and potentially causing water damage. Our technicians in Meridian are experts at identifying the source of leaks, whether it's a cracked tank or bowl, a faulty wax ring, or a worn-out fill valve. We use specialized dyes and pressure tests to accurately locate hidden leaks and provide durable repairs, often involving the replacement of seals, gaskets, or the entire flushing mechanism. Ignoring a leak can lead to structural damage or mold growth, so quick repair is crucial.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Meridian, MS

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Meridian

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Meridian, MS

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a new seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Meridian, MS

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush might mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are adept at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.
