    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot.     Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Meridian, MS

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Meridian

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Meridian

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.
