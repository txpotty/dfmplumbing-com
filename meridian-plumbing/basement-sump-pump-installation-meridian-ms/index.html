    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Meridian, MS

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. Those basements need a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower pump in a larger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Meridian

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Meridian homes. They are louder, though.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Meridian

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Meridian

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible. That is where a pedestal pump earns its place, and we install it with rigid discharge, a quality check valve, and a float rod set to your pit depth so it starts before water reaches the top of the liner. They are noisy but durable.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Meridian, MS

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Meridian, MS we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.
