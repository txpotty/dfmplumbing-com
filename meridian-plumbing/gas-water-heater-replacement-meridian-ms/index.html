    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Meridian

    The cost for gas water heater replacement in Meridian, MS typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the unit size, brand, efficiency and any needed plumbing changes. Factors like gas line upgrades or ventilation improvements can impact the final price.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing to remove sediment and checking the anode rod, can greatly extend your gas water heater's lifespan. Proper sizing and professional installation also play a crucial role.

    New gas water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. DFM Plumbing also provides a warranty on our installation workmanship, giving you extra peace of mind.

    Tankless can make sense if you want endless hot water and plan to stay long term, but upfront costs are higher. We can price both options so you can weigh them side by side.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to cleaner combustion.

    A typical gas water heater has a lifespan of 10 to 12 years with proper maintenance. Factors like water quality in Meridian, such as hard water causing mineral buildup, and usage patterns can affect its longevity.

    Water pooling at the base, rusty hot water, rumbling or popping sounds and a tank over 10 years old are common signs. A leak from the tank itself is not repairable.

    Most standard gas water heater replacements can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a power vent unit or relocating the water heater, may take longer, usually a full day.
