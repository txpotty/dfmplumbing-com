    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Meridian, MS

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Homes around Meridian on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a filter. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year.
