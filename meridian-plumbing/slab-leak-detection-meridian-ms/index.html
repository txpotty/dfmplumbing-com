    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak.     Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Meridian

    Our technicians rely on highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the faint sound of escaping water, even through concrete.     Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Meridian

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Meridian, MS

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic.
