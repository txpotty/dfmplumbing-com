    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Meridian

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an steady temperature. Water heaters in hot garages and attics wear faster, so their condition is worth checking during any plumbing visit. Properly working components are vital for both efficiency and safety.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Meridian, MS

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Meridian, MS

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Meridian

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Meridian, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Meridian

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.
