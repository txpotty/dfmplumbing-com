    Minor drain repairs, such as clearing a simple clog, typically do not require a permit in Meridian.     While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, we usually advise against using them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can damage your pipes, especially older ones, and are typically only a temporary fix. For effective and safe clog removal, professional methods are always recommended.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, effectively removing stubborn clogs, grease buildup and even tree roots. It's typically used when traditional snaking isn't enough, or for preventive maintenance on main sewer lines, especially in older homes with buildup issues.

    You should call a professional if your DIY efforts with a plunger or basic snake haven't cleared the clog, or if you have recurring clogs, foul odors or multiple slow drains. These issues usually point to a deeper problem that requires specialized tools and expertise to resolve effectively.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also rarely clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.

    We work with all common types of drain pipe found in Mississippi homes, including PVC, ABS, cast iron and copper. Our technicians are skilled in repairing and replacing sections of various pipe materials for compatibility and longevity with your existing plumbing system.

    To prevent future drain problems, avoid pouring grease down sinks, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles and be mindful of what goes down your toilets.
