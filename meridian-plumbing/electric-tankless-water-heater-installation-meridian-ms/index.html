    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. It is a short service.
