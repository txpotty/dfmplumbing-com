    What Goes Into a PEX Install in Brandon, MS

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Brandon, MS

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. While the studs are exposed we can run PEX back to a manifold or a sound trunk, set fresh stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a fridge line. An addition gets sized on its own. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Brandon

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Brandon, MS

    PEX rarely fails on its own. Most failures trace back to a nail or screw through the pipe, a rodent working on an attic run, or a fitting that was not crimped fully. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in Brandon, MS come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Brandon

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, though not the same pipe you drink from. Heating loops need oxygen-barrier PEX, which has a coating that keeps oxygen from migrating through the wall and rusting the boiler, the pump and any iron in the system. Loop length matters as well. Too long and the far end runs cold.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Brandon, MS

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Brandon

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    New Construction PEX Plumbing in Brandon, MS

    On a new build, DFM Plumbing runs PEX supply lines while the framing is still open in Brandon, MS. We drill the plates, set the manifold and stub out every fixture to the heights on the plans, then hold a pressure test for the rough-in inspection. Nail plates go on wherever pipe crosses a stud. Builders like the pace.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Brandon, MS

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. Cold weather slows it. We let joints set before testing.
