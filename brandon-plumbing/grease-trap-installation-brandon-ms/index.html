    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Brandon

    The cost of grease trap installation in Brandon, MS varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation. For a small under-sink hydro-mechanical unit, expect to pay between $1,500 and $4,000. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors can range from $7,000 to $20,000 or more, including excavation and concrete work.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent drain clogs, slow drainage in multiple fixtures, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or if your current unit is old, corroded, or no longer meeting local code requirements. An inspection by a professional plumber can confirm the need for replacement.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you choose the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.

    We install a variety of reputable grease trap brands, including Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, and Rockford Separators. Our plumbers are familiar with the specifications and installation requirements of numerous manufacturers, ensuring you get a high-quality, durable system tailored to your needs.

    The cleaning frequency for a new grease trap depends on its size, the volume of FOG produced by your kitchen, and local regulations in Brandon. Typically, grease traps require cleaning every 1 to 3 months. Regular cleaning is essential to maintain efficiency and prevent backups.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are typically rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' usually refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks. 'Grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor gravity-fed units with higher capacities. Both serve the same purpose of separating FOG from wastewater.

    DFM Plumbing provides a warranty on our workmanship for all grease trap installations. Additionally, the grease trap unit itself will usually come with a manufacturer's warranty. We will provide you with all warranty details at the time of installation, giving you peace of mind.
