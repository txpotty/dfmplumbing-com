    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Brandon

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor.     Leaking Disposal Fix in Brandon, MS

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out.     Noisy Disposal Repair in Brandon

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Brandon, MS

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Brandon

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.
