    Full Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Brandon, MS

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Brandon

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. PEX handles a freeze better than copper. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter open, so pressure at the far shower stays strong.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Brandon

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. After an emergency repair we schedule the full replacement, since removing the poly system is the only lasting fix.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Brandon

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Leaving some poly keeps the risk. New pipe goes from the main to every fixture and appliance, each one gets a new quarter-turn stop and the system is tested before any wall is closed.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Brandon

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. We still make them. We cut out the failed piece and splice in PEX with listed fittings, stopping the leak and giving you time to plan the full job.

    Polybutylene Replacement Costs in Brandon, MS

    Replacing polybutylene with PEX in an average home often costs $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size and bathrooms. Copper systems cost more. A yard service line typically adds $1,500 to $4,000, and that is still usually less than repairing damage from one burst.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Brandon

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Brandon, MS

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Polybutylene to PEX Transitions in Brandon, MS

    Sometimes the yard service line coming into the house is not polybutylene, or it was replaced already. In that case, we connect the new PEX system to the existing service at the main shutoff with an approved transition fitting, which avoids digging up the yard. The service line gets checked. If it is blue or black poly, we will recommend replacing it too.
