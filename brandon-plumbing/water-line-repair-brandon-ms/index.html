    Main Water Line Repair in Brandon

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause serious water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team specializes in diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Brandon

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Newer houses and manufactured homes typically rely on PEX or CPVC supply lines with PVC drains. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Brandon is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Brandon, MS

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Iron can leave rusty stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Brandon

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.
