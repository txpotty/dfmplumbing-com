    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Brandon

    The cost of water heater replacement in Brandon, MS usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and needed plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity affect the final price.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    DFM Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that fits your budget and hot water demands.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Iron can leave orange stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help lengthen that life.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment. It is a complex job best left to licensed professionals to ensure safety, proper installation and compliance with local plumbing codes, preventing potential leaks or hazards.
