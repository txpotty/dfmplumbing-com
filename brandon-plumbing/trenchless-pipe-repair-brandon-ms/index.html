    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Sectional Point Repair in Brandon

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Brandon laterals.

    Pipe Bursting in Brandon, MS

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.
