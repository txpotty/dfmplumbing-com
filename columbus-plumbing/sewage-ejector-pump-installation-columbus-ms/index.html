    Replacing a pump in an existing basin usually costs $900 to $2,000. A new ejector system with basin, venting and floor work usually ranges from $2,500 to $6,000 or more.

    Pump replacements typically take two to four hours. New systems with floor excavation and venting typically take one to two days.

    Most ejector pumps last 7 to 10 years with normal use. Flushing wipes or other non-flushable items can shorten that life considerably.

    Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves occasionally checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch. It is also wise to avoid flushing non-biodegradable items. DFM Plumbing offers maintenance services to keep your pump running efficiently and prevent surprise breakdowns.

    We mainly install high-quality, reputable brands such as Liberty Pumps and Zoeller. These manufacturers are known for their durability, reliability and efficient performance, providing a long-lasting solution for your home. We can discuss the best brand and model for your needs.
