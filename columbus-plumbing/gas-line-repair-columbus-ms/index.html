    Propane Gas Line Service in Columbus, MS

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Columbus, MS

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Columbus, MS

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Gas Leak Detection in Columbus

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. We isolate sections of the system and use electronic combustible gas detectors to narrow the source, then confirm with soap solution at fittings, valves and unions. Once we find it, you get a written repair price before work starts.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Columbus

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. Our plumbers total the BTU load, measure the run and size the pipe so the last appliance on the line still gets full pressure at peak use. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Columbus, MS

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Columbus, MS

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.
