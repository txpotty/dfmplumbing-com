    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, effectively softening the water.     Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or excessive soap use. Other signs are a noticeable drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.
