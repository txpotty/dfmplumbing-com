    Septic Pump and Alarm System Repair in Columbus

    Many modern septic systems, especially those on sloped properties or with advanced treatment units, rely on a working septic pump and alarm. When your pump fails or the alarm sounds, it indicates a serious issue. Our team can repair or replace faulty pumps, floats and electrical components so your system effectively moves wastewater to the drain field. We service all major pump brands for homes in Columbus.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Columbus

    Septic tank leaks can lead to unpleasant odors, soggy spots in your yard and potential groundwater contamination. Our technicians use dye testing and camera inspections to locate the source of the leak, whether it is a crack in the tank or a compromised pipe connection. We provide durable repair solutions using specialized sealants and patching materials designed for the harsh environment of a septic tank in Columbus, ensuring a watertight seal and preventing future issues.

    Septic Inlet Line Repair in Columbus

    The line from your house to the tank can crack, sag or separate from soil movement and roots. Symptoms include gurgling drains and backups even after pumping. We camera the line to locate the problem and repair or replace the damaged section. A cleanout near the tank makes future maintenance simpler.

    Effluent Pump Repair in Columbus, MS

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers trigger high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage comes back into the house.

    Alternative Septic System Repair in Columbus, MS

    Some properties use aerobic treatment units or other alternative systems because of soil or lot size limits. These systems have air pumps, timers and alarms that need routine service. We repair aerators, replace diffusers and troubleshoot control panels. We also help keep maintenance records required by your permit.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Columbus

    Sewage backing up into tubs or floor drains is a health hazard that needs prompt attention. We respond to emergency septic calls, find the blockage and restore flow. That might mean clearing a line, replacing a failed pump or arranging pumping if the tank is full. We then explain what caused the backup and how to avoid it.

    Leach Field Repair in Columbus, MS

    Soggy ground, strong odors or lush grass over the field can point to a struggling disposal area. Causes include clogged distribution pipes, a crushed distribution box or soil sealed by solids. We jet clean lines, replace distribution boxes and determine whether the field can be restored. If replacement is needed, we explain the design and permit steps.
