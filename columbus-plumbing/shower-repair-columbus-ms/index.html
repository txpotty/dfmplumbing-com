    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Columbus

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Columbus

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Columbus

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Columbus, MS

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.
