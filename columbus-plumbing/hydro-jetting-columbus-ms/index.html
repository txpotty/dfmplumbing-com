    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Columbus

    The cost of hydro jetting in Columbus, MS can range from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and whether camera inspection is required. More complex jobs, like removing extensive tree roots or cleaning long commercial lines, may be on the higher end. We provide a clear estimate after assessing your situation.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why DFM Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to assess your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters along with rotating and root-cutting nozzles from well-known manufacturers. Color camera systems record the line before and after.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are good reasons to consider jetting.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and wash debris away.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and damage fragile pipe.
