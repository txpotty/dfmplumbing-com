    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Columbus, MS

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Columbus, MS

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Columbus, MS

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Utility Sink Installation in Columbus

    Laundry and garage tubs take dirty water and a lot of it. We set the tub on a sturdy stand or wall bracket, run a 1-1/2 inch trap so sand and lint do not stop it up, and add a faucet that a bucket fits under. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. We add a cleanout plug.

    Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in Columbus

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. Wall-hung or countertop lavatories go on carriers with sensor faucets and mixing valves where tempered water is required. Vandal-resistant parts are available. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Columbus, MS

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Columbus, MS

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. Three-compartment sinks usually drain indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water within easy reach of every prep station on the line. Plan review comments come first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Columbus, MS

    New sinks often get a new disposal. The disposal goes on a new flange, the dishwasher knockout gets punched, and the outlet lines up above the trap. Forgetting the knockout plug floods the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Columbus

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. Stainless bowls with commercial faucets go in strong enough for a full bowl and a stack of dishes. We schedule around when you are open. Night installs are possible.
