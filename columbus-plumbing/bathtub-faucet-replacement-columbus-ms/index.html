    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Columbus, MS

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.
