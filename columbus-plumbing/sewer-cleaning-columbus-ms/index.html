    What Our Columbus, MS Sewer Techs Handle

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Columbus, MS

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Root Masses in Columbus Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Columbus

    Roots come back after cutting. Plan on regrowth in 12 to 18 months. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Columbus, MS

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Columbus, MS

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Columbus, MS

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Columbus

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. A sewer joint is a steady source. Our root saw cuts the mass loose, and heavy growth gets a jetter pass to clean out the fine hairs, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Columbus

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.
