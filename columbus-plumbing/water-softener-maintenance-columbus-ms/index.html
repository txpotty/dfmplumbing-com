    Control Valve and Injector Service in Columbus, MS

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Columbus, MS

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can build up in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and confirming the float valve operates correctly.     Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Columbus, MS

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for maximum softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.
