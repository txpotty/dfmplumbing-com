    Float Switch Checks in Columbus, MS

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Next we adjust the on and off levels so each run is longer and the starts are fewer.

    Wayne Sump Pump Service in Columbus, MS

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Columbus

    Hurricanes, tropical storms and heavy rain bring flooding risk to Mississippi, especially in low-lying and coastal areas. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Columbus, MS

    A new sump pump, installed, often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower, pit work and whether you add a battery backup. Routine maintenance costs a small fraction of that. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. You get a straight answer on whether your pump has life left or should be replaced.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Columbus

    Once a year is the minimum. Each yearly visit covers the motor, the impeller, the float and the discharge line. After that, we fill the basin and time the drawdown, since a slow pump-out is the first hint of a tired impeller or a restricted line.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Columbus

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. Then we run a full water test. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Columbus, MS

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. Often it is just a tripped GFCI or a hung float. A seized motor calls for a new pump, so we carry replacements and never leave the pit unprotected overnight.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Columbus, MS

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.
