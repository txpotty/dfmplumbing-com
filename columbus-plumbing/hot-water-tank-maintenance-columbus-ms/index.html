    Complete Hot Water Tank Maintenance Services in Columbus, MS

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Columbus

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is crucial for extending the life of your unit.

    Commercial and Rental Tank Maintenance in Columbus

    Property managers, restaurants and small offices often run several tanks that work much harder than a home unit. We set up scheduled maintenance for landlords and businesses, flushing each tank and logging the anode condition and valve test date. Records like these help with insurance and with planning replacements before a tank fails during business hours. We service 50 to 100 gallon commercial models as well as residential tanks in rental units.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Columbus, MS

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can ruin a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Columbus, MS

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it affects the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Columbus, MS

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.
