    Sump Pump Types We Replace in Columbus, MS

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Columbus

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. Before we set it, we make sure the float can swing freely without catching the wall.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Columbus

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Columbus, MS

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. Then we find out why it failed.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Columbus

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. Then a new check valve goes on. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Columbus, MS

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. Discharge goes away from the foundation. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Columbus, MS

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Columbus

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Columbus, MS

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Local Sump Pump Replacement in Columbus, MS

    Speed counts on a sump call. A basement can take on several inches of water in an hour when a pump quits during a heavy storm, so we send the closest available tech. Most arrive with the right pump.

    Groundwater levels shift with the seasons, and in a wet spring a pit can take on water every few minutes even with no rain in the forecast. Our sizing is based on the wettest week of the year. Not for an average day.
