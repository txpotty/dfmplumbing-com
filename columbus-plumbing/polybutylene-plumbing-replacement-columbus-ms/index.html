    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Columbus

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. PEX handles a freeze better than copper. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter open, so pressure at the far shower stays strong.

    Emergency Polybutylene Pipe Repair in Columbus, MS

    A burst poly fitting can push water across a floor or through a ceiling in minutes. Shut the main first. We stop the water, splice in a temporary repair to restore service and then plan the full repipe on a calm schedule. We take photos for the adjuster.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Columbus

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. Attic fittings fail hard. After an emergency repair we schedule the full replacement, since removing the poly system is the only lasting fix.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Columbus, MS

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. New PEX gets sized for the home and strapped to the frame. We check underneath. Freezing weather is infrequent in Mississippi, but a hard freeze every few winters can catch homes that were never built for it. Heat tape and insulation on exposed runs get inspected and replaced where needed, so the new supply lines are protected under the home.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Columbus, MS

    Some homeowners in Columbus, MS want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Partial Polybutylene Replacement in Columbus, MS

    Full replacement is the right long-term fix, but it is not always possible right away. Sometimes it has to wait. Replacing the riskiest parts first, near the heater and in the attic, lowers the odds of a burst while you plan the rest. We will be honest about what is left.

    Polybutylene Replacement Costs in Columbus, MS

    Replacing polybutylene with PEX in an average home often costs $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size and bathrooms. Copper systems cost more. A yard service line typically adds $1,500 to $4,000, and that is still usually less than repairing damage from one burst.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Columbus

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. You may also see it under sinks, near the main shutoff or coming through the garage wall, and we trace runs through the attic to confirm how much of the house it serves.
