    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Columbus

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Columbus

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Columbus, MS

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Columbus

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Columbus, MS

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Columbus

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. We set the carrier so the finished rim lands at 17 inches or lower, keep the clear floor space free of partitions and door swings, and mount the flush handle or sensor where a seated user can reach it. We measure the rim twice. Inspectors measure too.
