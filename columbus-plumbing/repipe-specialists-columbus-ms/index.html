    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Columbus, MS

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are notorious for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Columbus, MS has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent major water damage. DFM Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a reliable new system.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Columbus

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell later. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Columbus

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save money.

After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can decide.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Columbus, MS

    Iron can leave rusty stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, supporting a longer-lasting plumbing system.
