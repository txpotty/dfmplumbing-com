    PEX Repiping in Columbus

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. You also hear less of it, since PEX softens water hammer. It also handles a freeze better. Wrapping exposed lines and letting faucets drip on the coldest nights prevent most of the damage a sudden freeze can cause. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Columbus

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. A PEX repipe ends that cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Columbus

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. A certified tester checks each assembly on install day and the results go to the water provider. That starts the annual clock. Most jurisdictions want a retest every 12 months, and we can put you on a reminder list so it does not slip.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Columbus, MS

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. An ejector pump in a sealed basin lifts it up to the main drain. We size the pump and basin to the fixtures it serves, vent the basin to code, and put a check valve and a full-port shutoff on the discharge so the pump can be pulled for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Columbus, MS

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. Homes with a PRV usually need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Columbus

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. It keeps fertilizer-laced lawn water from being pulled back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops pressure. The type matters. We pick the assembly for the hazard level and local requirement and test it on install day.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Columbus, MS

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. CSST must be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Columbus

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. We size before we sell. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.
