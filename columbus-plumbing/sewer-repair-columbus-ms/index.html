    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Columbus

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address badly collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Mississippi properties. Our team makes sure of careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Columbus.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Columbus, MS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can crack drain lines and supply pipes. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Columbus, MS

    When repairs are no longer enough due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary. We offer several replacement options, including traditional trenching and modern trenchless technologies like pipe bursting or pipe lining. Our experts will assess your situation and recommend the most cost-effective and least disruptive option for your Mississippi property, giving you a long-lasting new sewer line.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Columbus

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Columbus, MS

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Columbus.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Columbus

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is highly effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Mississippi homes.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Columbus

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.
