    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Columbus

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Columbus

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Columbus, MS

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Columbus

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Columbus, MS

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. Each brand uses its own part number. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Columbus, MS

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. We bring the common ones. For filtered water faucets, we replace the cartridge or the check valve as needed.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Columbus, MS

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Columbus, MS

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.
