    Grease Trap Fixes We Handle in Columbus, MS

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Columbus

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Both require a full pump-out and a dry tank.

    Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Columbus

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. We see undersized traps fail in the same few places every time. The inlet tee clogs, the outlet tee gets coated, and grease starts showing up in the sewer manhole down the street, which is usually how the city finds out before the owner does. We repair what broke, then tell you straight whether the unit is sized for your volume.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Columbus

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Columbus, MS

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. A kitchen running two fryers can hit that mark in a couple of weeks.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Columbus

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. Lines between the sinks and the trap clog first, because that is where hot water cools. We put a camera in from the nearest cleanout and record the run, looking for sags, offset joints, roots in clay sections downstream, or a belly where grease collects every week. Jetting a belly buys time, but only regrading the pipe fixes it.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Columbus

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. With old cast iron, we turn the pressure down and keep a camera on it, since a thin wall can blow out.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Columbus, MS

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. A cracked riser lets surface water and grit into the interceptor. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. A light-duty lid in a drive aisle will break eventually.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Columbus

    A sewer smell near the trap means gas is getting out somewhere it should not. Most of the time it is a torn lid gasket or a dry floor drain nearby. We check venting on the fixture side too, because a sink with no vent can siphon its own trap seal and let sewer gas back into the kitchen. Pumping the trap helps for a week, but it will not fix a gasket that no longer seals.
