    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Columbus

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. One offset joint near the house is a repair. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet tell us the line is failing everywhere, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Columbus, MS

    Diagnosis comes before price. The camera shows what is wrong, the locator shows where. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, then camera again so we are looking at the pipe itself. Guesswork costs money.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Columbus, MS

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. A hairline crack is enough. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and cutting them out with a machine only buys a few months, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Columbus, MS

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Columbus

    Brittle pipe bursts cleanly. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind so the new pipe has no joints at all between the pits. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Columbus, MS

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. CIPP lining coats the inside of the existing pipe instead. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, since it puts a new pipe in place instead of reinforcing the old, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Columbus

    A sewer camera on a push cable goes in through the cleanout. The footage shows each joint, crack and root intrusion. A small transmitter called a sonde sits behind the camera head, so we can find its exact spot and depth from the yard above, which keeps the digging small. Bring questions.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Columbus, MS

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop using sinks, toilets and the washer. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and when the pipe has caved in, we dig to that spot immediately, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.
