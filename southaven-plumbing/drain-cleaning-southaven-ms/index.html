    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in Southaven

    Standard clogs in Southaven, MS run about $150 to $500. Main line work is priced higher. Hydro jetting or a heavy main line blockage can run $400 to $800 or a bit more if there is no cleanout, and we price it after seeing the job.

    No permit is needed for routine drain cleaning. Pipe work can be another story. Replacing a sewer line, adding a cleanout or changing the drain layout usually needs a permit, and we tell you upfront if your job falls into that group.

    Fixture drains: 30 to 60 minutes. Plan on 1 to 2 hours for a main, more if we have to pull a toilet or cut through a heavy root mass.

    Water backing up into a different fixture is the giveaway. That means a shared line is blocked. A single slow sink is usually a trap or a short branch, but a toilet that bubbles when the tub drains means the blockage is farther down.

    Roots follow moisture. A sewer line leaks a little vapor at every loose joint. Hair-thin roots slip in, then grow into a mass that snags everything flushed down the line, and clay and older cast iron lines near mature trees are the most common victims.

    Enzyme products are fine for upkeep. The harsh ones are risky. If a caustic cleaner fails, the next person to open that drain is working in a trap full of chemical.

    Jetting makes sense for repeat clogs. It works best on buildup. Snaking punches through, but jetting cleans the full circumference of the pipe, so the line stays clear longer.

    Not all. Healthy pipe is not hurt by it. Badly corroded cast iron, Orangeburg or clay with broken joints can be damaged, which is why we run a camera before jetting any older line in town.
