    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Southaven, MS

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Southaven, MS

Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units.     Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Southaven

    Many homes in Southaven have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Southaven

    Shower remodels that change plumbing or drain locations often require a permit. Simple membrane replacement may not. We check local requirements and pull permits when needed.

    Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also suggest water is getting behind.

    A shower wall waterproofing project typically takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take several days.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, typically managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    Skilled DIYers can roll on liquid membranes, but mistakes at seams, corners and drains often lead to leaks. Shower pans also require correct slope and flood testing. Professional installation reduces the risk of hidden water damage.
