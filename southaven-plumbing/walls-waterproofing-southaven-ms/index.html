    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Southaven, MS

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Southaven

    Some homes from the 1980s and 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which is known for sudden leaks and is usually replaced. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Southaven, MS

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Southaven, MS

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Southaven

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that picks up the cooler wet area, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Southaven

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Southaven

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Low, wet ground in many areas also means poor drainage, which matters for sewer lines and septic fields alike. Backfill goes in last.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Southaven, MS

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Damp proofing is usually a thin spray-on asphalt coat. It slows soil moisture but will not stop water that is pushing against the wall under pressure. Waterproofing uses thicker membranes, drainage boards and footing drains rated to stop liquid water, which is what a basement in wet soil or a high water table needs.

    We do. Our workmanship is warrantied, and a lot of membrane systems also have a manufacturer warranty when they are installed to spec, which is one more reason we never skip a step on cure time or thickness. Your written quote explains the coverage.

    Sprinklers, nine times out of ten. Spray heads and drip lines aimed at the house soak the block every day, and soil sloped toward the wall or a short downspout adds to it, so the masonry stays wet and leaves mineral deposits as it dries. Moving one sprinkler head can fix it.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers.
