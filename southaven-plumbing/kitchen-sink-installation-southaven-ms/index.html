    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Southaven, MS: The Details

    Apron-Front Sinks in Southaven, MS

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Southaven, MS

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks each have their own health code rules. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. We confirm the rules first. Work is scheduled before or after service so the kitchen does not close.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Southaven, MS

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. A deep bowl means a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Southaven

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Southaven, MS

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. You also need power. There needs to be a switched outlet under the sink.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Southaven

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. Usually the fastest upgrade. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Southaven

    A cracked bowl or a rusted-through strainer can soak a cabinet in an afternoon. First, close both angle stops and stop using the sink. Common stainless sizes ride on the truck. If you have a standard 33 by 22 inch drop-in, we can usually remove the damaged sink and set a new one that day.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Southaven

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Then come the strainers and trap.
