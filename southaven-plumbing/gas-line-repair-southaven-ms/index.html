    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Southaven, MS

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Southaven, MS. Inspection covers sizing, approved materials, pipe supports, shutoffs, drip legs and a witnessed or documented pressure test. The permit is on us. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Southaven

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    CSST Repair in Southaven

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. Bonding gets checked on every system. The system should be bonded to the electrical grounding system with a proper clamp and conductor, per the manufacturer's instructions and local code.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Southaven

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Southaven, MS

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Southaven, MS

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Southaven

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Southaven, MS

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. Utilities get marked first. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.
