    Water-efficient toilets, often labeled WaterSense, use 1.28 gallons per flush (GPF) or less, compared to older models that can use 3.5 GPF or more. They save a large amount of water and can lower your utility bills, which benefits any household.

    If your toilet is older than 20 years, uses a lot of water or the bowl is hard to source, replacing the whole toilet is often smarter. If the tank is newer and works well, a bowl swap can save money.

    A wax ring is a crucial seal located at the base of your toilet bowl, creating a watertight barrier between the toilet and the drain pipe.     Homes around Southaven on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a treatment system.
