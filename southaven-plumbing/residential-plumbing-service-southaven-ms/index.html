    Residential Plumbing Service in Southaven, MS: What We Fix

    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Southaven

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Expansive clay soils are common in Mississippi, swelling with rain and shrinking in dry spells, which puts stress on foundations and buried pipes. We locate them with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Drain Cleaning and Sewer Camera Service in Southaven

    Wipes are not flushable, whatever the package says. Kitchen grease, hair and wipes cause most slow drains, and for a main line backup we cable it open, then run a camera to look for roots, offset joints or a belly where water sits in the pipe. You get the video.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Southaven

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. Our plumbers swap failing pumps for cast iron submersibles and replace stuck floats and check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. You can test yours with a five-gallon bucket of water.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping in Southaven, MS

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. We patch single leaks with a copper repair or PEX transition. More than one leak usually means repiping. Freeze splits get the same treatment, cut out and replaced with new pipe rated for the job.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in Southaven, MS

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. We size the pipe for the appliance's BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. Then we light it and check the flame.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Southaven

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. The usual clues are a warm patch of floor, a meter dial moving with everything off, or a sudden jump in the water bill. We pinpoint it before quoting. Repeat leaks usually point to a repipe instead of another patch.

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Southaven

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. The truck has cartridges for the big brands, Moen and Delta included, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old stops fail when touched.

    Water Softener and Filtration Service in Southaven

    Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We put in and maintain whole-house softeners and carbon filters, including Kinetico and Pelican units, sized from a hardness test we run at your kitchen sink, not a guess. Then we set the regeneration schedule.
