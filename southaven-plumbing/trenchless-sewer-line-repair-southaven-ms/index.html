    Sectional Point Repair in Southaven

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. We push it in on a packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. One crack, one patch. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Trenchless Repair in Southaven

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The monitor shows us the pipe wall in real time, along with the footage counter, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. The locator also reads depth, which matters for pit planning. No guessing where to dig.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Pricing in Southaven, MS

    Compare total cost, not just the plumbing line on the invoice. A dig job looks cheaper on paper, until you add saw-cutting the driveway and pouring new concrete over a trench that may run 50 feet. Trenchless avoids most of that. Sod and sprinkler repair add up too.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Southaven, MS

    Depth changes the math. A shallow line in open lawn is cheap to dig, but a lateral eight feet deep that runs under the driveway and a porch costs far more to excavate than to burst or line from two pits. You get both prices on paper. Then you pick.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Southaven, MS

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once in, they fill the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. With no joints left, there is nowhere for them to return.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Southaven

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Southaven, MS

    Restaurants feel a sewer failure fast, because grease and a dish machine put heavy load on a 4- or 6-inch building drain all day. Lining can be scheduled overnight or on a day you are closed. A trench through a parking lot means lost spaces and a blocked entrance for a week or more. Liners skip that.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Prep and Lining in Southaven

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line to remove roots and grease, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.
