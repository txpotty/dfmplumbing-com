    Sump Pump Maintenance in Southaven, MS: Pits, Floats and Valves

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Southaven

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. That debris gets pulled into the intake and jams the impeller. Our plumber pumps the basin down, rinses the screen and inspects the liner for cracks that let dirt seep in through the walls. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Southaven

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Southaven, MS

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Southaven

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Southaven

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Southaven

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. We send a plumber with floats, check valves and spare pumps on the truck. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Southaven

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Southaven, MS

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.
