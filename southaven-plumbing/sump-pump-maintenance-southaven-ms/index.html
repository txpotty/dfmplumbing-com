    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Southaven, MS

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Southaven, MS.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Southaven

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Southaven, MS

    For Wayne sump pump owners in Southaven, MS, DFM Plumbing offers professional maintenance. Our technicians are familiar with the features of Wayne pumps, including their top-suction design and specific float switch mechanisms. We conduct thorough inspections to make sure the pump's intake is clear, the impeller is free of debris and all connections are secure, maximizing its efficiency and preventing clogs.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Southaven

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Southaven due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, preventing electrical hazards and supporting longevity.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Southaven

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Southaven, MS

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Southaven

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Southaven residence.
