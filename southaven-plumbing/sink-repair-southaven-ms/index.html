    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Southaven, MS

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Southaven

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. The overflow channel gets flushed too. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Southaven, MS

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Southaven

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Exposed pipes in crawlspaces, outdoor spigots and well equipment are the most vulnerable during a rare deep cold snap.     Garbage Disposal Repair in Southaven
