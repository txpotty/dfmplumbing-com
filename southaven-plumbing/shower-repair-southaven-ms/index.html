    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Southaven, MS

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Southaven

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. The drain gets cleared and tested. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Southaven, MS

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Southaven

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. You can step up to a thermostatic valve for tighter control.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Southaven

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Southaven, MS

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Southaven, MS

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.
