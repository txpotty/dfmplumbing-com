    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Southaven, MS

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Southaven, MS

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Southaven, MS

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. Put the unit in bypass first. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Southaven

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Use pellet salt.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Southaven

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Or the whole system.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Southaven

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Southaven, MS

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Southaven, MS

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.
