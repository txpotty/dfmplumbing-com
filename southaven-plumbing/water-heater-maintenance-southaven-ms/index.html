    Tankless Descaling in Southaven, MS

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. A pump runs descaler through the exchanger for the recommended time, followed by a clean water flush. The inlet screen gets cleaned too. Igniter, burner and venting get a safety check.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Southaven

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. We check the pan and drain. Access platforms and venting get looked at too.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Southaven

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Bad venting is dangerous. The gas line and shutoff get leak tested.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Southaven

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Maintenance for Every Brand in Southaven

    Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz, Rinnai and more. We follow each manufacturer's procedures so the service supports any warranty claim. Model details matter. We bring the right parts.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Southaven, MS

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. Leaky valves get replaced. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.

    Anode Rod Checks in Southaven

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. It comes out for inspection. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.
