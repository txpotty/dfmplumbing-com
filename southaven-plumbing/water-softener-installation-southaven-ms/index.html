    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Southaven

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Southaven

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Southaven

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Southaven, MS

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Filters add cost. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Southaven, MS

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. We remove the old unit, check the loop and drain for problems and install a newer model that fits your current hardness and household size. Disposal is included. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Southaven

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Southaven

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Southaven, MS

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.
