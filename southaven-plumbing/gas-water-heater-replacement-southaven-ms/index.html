    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Southaven, MS Home

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Southaven, MS

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. Choosing is simple.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Southaven, MS

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes last longest. Your plans for the house help decide it.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Southaven

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. New flex connectors every time. We soap-test the gas joints and check the draft hood with the burner lit.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Southaven, MS

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Southaven

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Southaven, MS

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in Southaven

    Laundromats and multi-unit buildings work commercial tanks so hard that replacement comes on a predictable schedule rather than as a surprise. We plan the swap around your slow hours. Venting and gas work may be extra.

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Mississippi plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.
