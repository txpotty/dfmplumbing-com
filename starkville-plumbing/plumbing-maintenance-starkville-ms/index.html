    Backflow Preventer Testing in Starkville

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Yearly tests are often required, and our certified testers check each valve and file results. Failed devices get rebuilt. Weather protection gets checked too. Late tests can mean fines.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Starkville

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Starkville, MS

    Flash flooding can overwhelm storm drains and push sewage up through sewer lines, particularly in older neighborhoods. A sump pump sits idle for months and then has to work hard all at once, so we test the float switch, clean debris out of the pit and confirm the discharge line runs clear to daylight. The check valve gets a look. Battery backups get tested too.

    Hidden Leak Checks in Starkville, MS

    A leak you cannot see still costs money. We shut off every fixture and watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic and thermal tools to track hidden leaks in walls, under slabs and in buried lines. Some homes from the 1980s and 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to sudden leaks and is usually replaced. Catching it early limits damage. Usually the fix is minor.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Starkville, MS

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. We check visible gas piping and appliance connectors for corrosion and leaks, confirm the shutoff valves turn, and note any old flexible connectors that should be replaced before they crack. Pipe material matters too. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Starkville

    Some homes from the 1980s and 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to sudden leaks and is usually replaced. Buried and under-slab water lines can wear from soil movement and hard water until they start leaking where nobody can see it, sometimes for months. Warm floors are a clue. A spinning meter is another. We use acoustic and thermal tools to find the spot.

    Softener Upkeep in Starkville, MS

    Softeners fail quietly. A softener that quit regenerating still sends water, just hard water, and you only notice when scale appears. Bypass and drain get tested. We measure hardness after.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Starkville, MS

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Mineral buildup on the bottom wastes fuel and can lead to early tank failure.
