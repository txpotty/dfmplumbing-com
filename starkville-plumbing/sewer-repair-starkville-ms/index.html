    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Starkville

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Starkville, MS

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to serious damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. DFM Plumbing offers emergency repair services to quickly address these critical failures.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Starkville, MS

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Starkville.
