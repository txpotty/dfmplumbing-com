    How We Repair Basement Leaks in Starkville, MS

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Starkville

    A floor drain that backs up when the washer drains or during a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. We cable or jet the line, then camera it to confirm. Where the city sewer surcharges in storms, a backwater valve on the building drain keeps sewage from coming up through the floor drain, and it needs its flapper cleaned once a year. Dry traps smell.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Starkville

    Water lines along a basement ceiling can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. We cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, and if we find more than two or three pinholes on one run, we recommend repiping that run instead of patching it again. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Starkville, MS

    Damp walls without a crack usually mean water moving through porous concrete or block. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Real seepage calls for better drainage or an interior channel.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Starkville, MS

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale from the inside out. A wet spot at the base of the stack or a crack along the hub is common, and we replace the damaged section with PVC or new cast iron joined with no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. Loose PVC joints usually trace back to poor support or a fitting that was never fully seated.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Starkville

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can crack drain lines and supply pipes. We look at gutters and grading first, since fixing them outside often stops the seepage for far less money. Cheap fixes come first.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Starkville

    Commercial basements hold inventory, records or mechanical rooms that cannot sit in water. We look at floor drains, elevator pits and sump systems as one system, since a single failed check valve or a clogged pit inlet can flood the whole lower level. Duplex pumps help. A high-water alarm warns staff before the floor gets wet.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Relief in Starkville

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.
