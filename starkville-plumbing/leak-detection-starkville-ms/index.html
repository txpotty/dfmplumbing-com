    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Starkville, MS

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, and on plastic pipe we inject tracer gas and sniff for it at the surface.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Starkville, MS

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, with a locator giving the exact depth and distance.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Starkville

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Pool Leak Detection in Starkville

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, use an underwater hydrophone to listen, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    Leak Testing for Multifamily and Offices in Starkville

    Multifamily buildings and office parks have long supply systems that let leaks hide for months. We isolate each unit or floor, test it and trace leaks with the same acoustic and thermal tools we use in single-family houses. Property managers get a report per unit. Early detection means fewer tenant complaints and fewer insurance claims building-wide.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Starkville, MS

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves.     Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Starkville
