    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Starkville, MS

    Leaks in underground water lines, often leading from the meter to your home, can result in significant water loss and property damage. We employ ground microphones and tracer gas detection to locate these buried leaks precisely. Factors like tree roots, ground shifting and corrosion in older galvanized pipes can contribute to these issues, making professional detection essential.

    Pool Leak Detection in Starkville

    A leaking pool can lead to wasted water and higher utility bills. Our specialists are experienced in detecting leaks in pool liners, plumbing and equipment. We use dye testing, pressure testing and listening devices to find the precise source of the leak, whether it's in the skimmer, main drain or return lines. Protect your investment and enjoy your pool without endless refilling in Starkville.

    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Starkville, MS

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to extensive drywall damage, wood rot and mold. We use non-invasive methods like moisture meters, thermal cameras and borescopes to accurately pinpoint the source of these leaks. This approach minimizes the need for destructive investigation, saving you time and money on repairs to your home's interior.

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Starkville

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, unusually green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, targeted hole.

    Slab Leak Detection in Starkville

    Slab leaks are a common concern for homes with concrete foundations, especially where soils shift. Our technicians use dedicated acoustic equipment and thermal cameras to pinpoint leaks under your home's foundation without destructive digging. Early detection of a slab leak prevents serious structural damage and mold issues, saving you significant repair costs down the line. We can locate leaks in both hot and cold water lines beneath the slab.

    Irrigation and Yard Leak Detection in Starkville, MS

    Drip and sprinkler systems are a common source of hidden water loss in most yards. A cracked lateral or a stuck valve can waste thousands of gallons before anyone notices. We test the irrigation system separately from the house and trace leaks along buried lines. Many water providers recommend checking irrigation first when bills jump in summer.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leak Detection in Starkville

    Leaks inside walls and ceilings often show up as stains, bubbling paint, soft drywall or a musty smell. We use thermal cameras to spot temperature differences caused by water, then confirm with moisture meters. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line. Pinpointing the source lets us cut a small, neat access opening.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Starkville

    Summer heat and wind can evaporate a lot of pool water, so it can be hard to tell normal loss from a leak. A bucket test compares evaporation to actual water loss. We then pressure test plumbing lines and use dye testing around lights, skimmers and fittings. Finding the leak protects the pool shell and the soil around it.
