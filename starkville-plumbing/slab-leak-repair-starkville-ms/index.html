    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Starkville, MS

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most efficient solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the damaged section. Our technicians are skilled in precise concrete cutting and restoration, so the repair is effective and the area is properly restored. We use durable materials designed to hold up to soil movement under the slab.

    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Starkville, MS

    Slab leaks aren't always about clean water pipes, since sewer lines can also leak under your foundation. These leaks pose serious health risks and can cause extensive damage. We specialize in detecting and repairing sewer line slab leaks, using camera inspections to identify the exact problem area. Our methods restore proper waste removal and prevent contamination in your Starkville, MS property, following health and safety codes.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Starkville

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Starkville

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Starkville with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Starkville, MS

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Slab Leak Detection in Starkville

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to determine which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.
