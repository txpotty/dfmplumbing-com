    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can crack drain lines and supply pipes.     Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Starkville

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Starkville yard.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Starkville

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Starkville

    Older Starkville homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.
