    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Choices in Starkville, MS

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Starkville, MS

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Starkville, MS

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Starkville, MS

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Starkville, MS

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. Inside, rust builds once the coating fails. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, while threaded joints corrode through first. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Starkville

    Apartments and condos share risers. One leak can affect several units. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, notify tenants ahead of shutoffs, and test each section before moving on. Organized work.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Starkville

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Exposed pipes in crawlspaces, outdoor spigots and well equipment are the first to fail during a rare deep cold snap. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Starkville, MS

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Close the main shutoff right away. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and our crews aim to stop it fast. Drying comes next.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Starkville

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.
