    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Starkville, MS

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.
