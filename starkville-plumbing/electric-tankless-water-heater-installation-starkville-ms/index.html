    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Starkville

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Iron can leave rusty stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. It is a short service.
