    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Starkville

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are different. DFM Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in Starkville. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly misinstallations.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Starkville

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so toilets, showers and sinks drain into a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We excavate the floor, set a basin usually 30 to 50 gallons and connect each fixture drain. A solids-handling pump lifts waste up and into the main drain line. The basin lid is sealed and vented to keep odors from escaping.
