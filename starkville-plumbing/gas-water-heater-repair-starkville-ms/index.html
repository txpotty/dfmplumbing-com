    Propane Water Heater Repair in Starkville

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane rather than natural gas. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.

    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Starkville

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. While some leaks may call for a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired.
