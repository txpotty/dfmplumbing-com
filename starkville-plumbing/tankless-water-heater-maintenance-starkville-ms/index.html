    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Starkville

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Starkville

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also confirm a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Starkville homes.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in Starkville, MS

    Noritz tankless water heaters are sturdy systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season. Our maintenance includes checking the unit's exhaust system, ensuring proper combustion air intake and verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger.
