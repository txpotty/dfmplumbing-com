    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Ridgeland, MS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
