    Water Line Materials We Install in Ridgeland, MS

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each has a place.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Ridgeland, MS

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and swap the old gate valve for a full-port ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Ridgeland, MS

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is lighter and mostly goes indoors. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against corrosive soil and stray rocks. Same fittings, different pipe.
