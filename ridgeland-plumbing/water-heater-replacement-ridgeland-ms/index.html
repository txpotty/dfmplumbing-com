    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Ridgeland

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and typical code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. DFM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.

    Yes, in many areas of Ridgeland and Mississippi, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement to make sure the installation meets local safety and building codes. DFM Plumbing handles all required permitting on your behalf, so you do not have to worry about compliance.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from impacting heating elements.

    In general, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a viable option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take more time.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a licensed plumber.
