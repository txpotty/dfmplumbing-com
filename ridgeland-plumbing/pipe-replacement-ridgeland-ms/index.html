    PEX Pipe Replacement in Ridgeland

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Freezing weather is infrequent in Mississippi, but a hard freeze every few winters can catch homes that were never built for it. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. DFM Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.
