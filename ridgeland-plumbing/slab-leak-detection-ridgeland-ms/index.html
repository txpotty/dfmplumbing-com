    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Ridgeland, MS

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Ridgeland

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Ridgeland, MS

    One leak on healthy pipe is a good spot-repair candidate. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, replace the bad section and hold a pressure test. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. Accurate marks keep that hole small.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Ridgeland, MS

    Older homes in Ridgeland, MS often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. Our techs know where these fail. That knowledge points the search at the likeliest spots first.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Ridgeland

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, listening for the spot where the sound peaks and then checking it again from a different angle, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.
