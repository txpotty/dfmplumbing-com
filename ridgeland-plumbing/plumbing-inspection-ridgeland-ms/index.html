    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Ridgeland

    Iron can leave rusty stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Ridgeland, MS

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Ridgeland

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Ridgeland, MS

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Ridgeland, MS

    Older homes in Ridgeland often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. Our camera shows each defect, and the onboard counter tells us exactly how far from the cleanout it sits. We mark it on the ground.
