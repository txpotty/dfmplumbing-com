    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Ridgeland

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds.     Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Ridgeland

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than you might think. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Ridgeland, MS

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.
