    Picking the Right Softener for Ridgeland, MS Homes

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Ridgeland, MS

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Mississippi homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. The drain line gets an air gap.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Ridgeland

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Ridgeland

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. They cost more.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Ridgeland

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. Peak flow drives the sizing. We work around your business hours.

    Softener Loop Installation in Ridgeland, MS

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Ridgeland

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Ridgeland

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. Sizing comes from peak flow and daily use. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Ridgeland, MS

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Mississippi homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.
