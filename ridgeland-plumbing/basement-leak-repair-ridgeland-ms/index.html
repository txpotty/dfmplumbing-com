    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Ridgeland

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. A jetter usually opens it. Crushed clay tile has to be dug up and replaced with perforated PVC or corrugated pipe in a sock.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Ridgeland

    A floor drain that backs up when the washer drains or during a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. We cable or jet the line, then camera it to confirm. In homes where the street sewer backs up in storms, a backwater valve stops sewage from rising through the floor drain. Dry traps smell.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Ridgeland, MS

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Ridgeland, MS

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes rust through from the inside over decades. We cut out cracked cast iron and splice in new pipe with shielded no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. A separated PVC joint usually means the pipe was not fully seated or not supported.

    Exterior Membrane Waterproofing in Ridgeland, MS

    Excavating the outside of a foundation puts the repair where the water actually gets in. Long, hot and humid summers are the main stress on plumbing in Mississippi, and humidity keeps crawlspaces damp for much of the year. We pick a membrane rated for that exposure, lay new perforated drain pipe at the footing in clean stone, and backfill so the grade slopes away from the house at least six inches over the first ten feet. Downspouts get extended too.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Ridgeland, MS

    Water pressing against the outside of a basement wall finds the weakest point, usually the cove joint where wall meets floor. Flash flooding can overwhelm storm drains and push sewage up through sewer lines, particularly in older neighborhoods. An interior drain channel along that joint relieves the pressure by giving the water a path to the sump. No pressure, no seepage.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Ridgeland

    A sump pump lives in a pit cut below floor level and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.
