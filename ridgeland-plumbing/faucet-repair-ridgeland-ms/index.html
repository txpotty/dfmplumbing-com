    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Ridgeland, MS

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Ridgeland, MS

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. Ask us to check. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Ridgeland

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Ridgeland

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Exposed pipes in crawlspaces, outdoor spigots and well equipment are the most vulnerable during a rare deep cold snap. We replace packing washers and seat washers on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Ridgeland, MS

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. The aerator comes off with a key and gets cleaned or replaced, then check the cartridge screens. Low flow everywhere is a different problem.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Ridgeland, MS

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. Spouts install two ways. We measure the pipe stub and bring the right style, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Ridgeland, MS

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Ridgeland

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.
