    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Ridgeland, MS

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Ridgeland, MS

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Ridgeland, MS

    If your sump pump is acting up, making unusual noises or not turning on, our emergency diagnostic service can quickly identify the problem. We use specialized tools to troubleshoot electrical issues, motor failures and float switch malfunctions. Fast diagnosis and repair are essential to prevent water buildup, especially during unpredictable rainy seasons in Ridgeland, MS.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Ridgeland, MS

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff. We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. Exposed pipes in crawlspaces, outdoor spigots and well equipment are the most vulnerable during a rare deep cold snap.
