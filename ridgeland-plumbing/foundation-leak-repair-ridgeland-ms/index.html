    Foundation Leak Detection in Ridgeland

    Accurate detection is the key step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to verify a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Ridgeland

    Many foundation leaks start from exterior factors like poor drainage, overflowing gutters, or saturated soil. We assess your home's exterior to identify these culprits. Our solutions include grading adjustments, French drain installation, and downspout extensions to redirect water away from your foundation. Good exterior water management is key for preventing hydrostatic pressure buildup against your foundation walls in Ridgeland and keeping water out.
