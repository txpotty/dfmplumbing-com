    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Ridgeland, MS

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Ridgeland, MS

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Ridgeland

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Ridgeland can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
