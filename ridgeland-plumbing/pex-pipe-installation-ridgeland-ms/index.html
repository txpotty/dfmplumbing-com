    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Ridgeland, MS

    New Construction PEX Plumbing in Ridgeland, MS

    On a new build, DFM Plumbing runs PEX supply lines while the framing is still open in Ridgeland, MS.     Uponor PEX System Installation in Ridgeland

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Ridgeland

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. We replace every foot of it with PEX. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Ridgeland, MS

    Think of a manifold as a breaker panel for water. A central header feeds each fixture on its own line with its own valve, which lets you shut off one toilet and leave the kitchen running. You buy more pipe. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Ridgeland, MS

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines. With the studs bare, we can run PEX back to a manifold or to the nearest good trunk, set new stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a line for the fridge ice maker. Additions get their own sizing. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Ridgeland

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Ridgeland, MS

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Ridgeland, MS

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Many insurers ask whether you have it. Replacing it with PEX can make the house easier to insure and sell.
