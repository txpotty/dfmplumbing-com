    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Pascagoula

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It splits suddenly. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, and buried runs tend to split at fittings or where the pipe was bent tight during installation. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Burst Main Line Repair in Pascagoula, MS

    A ruptured main can flood a yard or leave you with no water at all. Find the meter valve. We help you close the meter valve over the phone, then send a crew to locate and repair the break, while sorting out with the utility whether the leak is on their side. Updates come as we go.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Pascagoula

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, which is why we recommend a full swap to PEX or copper threaded along the existing route. Pressure comes back right away.

    Commercial Underground Pipe Repair in Pascagoula

    Commercial properties run larger and longer buried lines. Losses add up fast. We locate leaks under parking lots, sidewalks and landscaped common areas carefully so concrete cutting stays to the minimum and tenants keep access to the building. After-hours work is available.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Pascagoula, MS

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Buried Sewer Lateral Repair in Pascagoula, MS

    Buried laterals crack from roots, settling and age. The camera finds the spot. We excavate at the mark, cut out the damaged length and replace it with Schedule 40 PVC, joining it to the existing pipe with shielded couplings sized for the outside diameter of each material. We confirm slope before backfill.

    Foundation Leak Detection and Repair in Pascagoula, MS

    A leak under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor. Or a hissing sound. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging, choosing between a targeted floor cut and a reroute through the walls or attic. We explain the tradeoffs.

    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Pascagoula

    Digging a trench across a mature yard is expensive to restore. Trenchless avoids most of it. We open small access pits at each end, then pull new pipe through the old path or line the old pipe in place, which saves sod, irrigation, pavers and concrete that would otherwise have to be torn out and replaced. Most jobs finish sooner.
