    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Pascagoula

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Pascagoula, MS

    Homes around Pascagoula on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a treatment system. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Pascagoula, MS

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.
