    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Pascagoula, MS

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Pascagoula and can often be finished faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Pascagoula, MS

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods greatly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Pascagoula, MS while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Pascagoula, MS

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is usually the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life.
