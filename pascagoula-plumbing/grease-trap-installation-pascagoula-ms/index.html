    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Pascagoula, MS

    Price follows size and location more than brand. A small indoor trap set on an open floor near the sink is the cheapest version of this job. Outdoor tanks with digging and a permit sit at the top of the range. We quote after a site visit.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Pascagoula

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. We pipe the air intake the way the manufacturer and the code both require. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. A trap that sits out of level loses capacity, so we shim it flat before connecting anything.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Pascagoula

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. Wastewater slows down in the tank long enough for grease to float and solids to settle. Sizing comes from your fixture units and the retention time your sewer authority wants, and we check that number with them before ordering a tank. No moving parts to fail.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Pascagoula

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. Before anything ships, we check that the tank and covers carry an H-20 traffic rating where trucks will roll over them. Most go in over two to four days. We set the inlet a few inches higher than the outlet and check both with a laser before any dirt goes back in.

    Commercial Grease Trap Installation in Pascagoula

    A commercial kitchen with a three-compartment sink, a pre-rinse station and two floor sinks can push 50 gallons a minute of greasy water at the dinner rush. We size the trap to that peak flow, not the average. Undersized traps are the usual failure. The install covers setting the unit, piping the inlet and outlet, and adding the vented flow control the maker specifies.

    Restaurant Grease Trap Compliance in Pascagoula, MS

    Most sewer authorities run a FOG program with an inspector who opens your trap and checks your pump-out manifests against the schedule they set. A failed inspection can bring a fine or an order to upgrade. We install to pass. That means a sample port where the program wants one and a unit sized to the number their formula gives, not the number the last contractor guessed.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Pascagoula

    On a new build-out, the grease trap gets decided before the slab is poured, not after. We work from the architect's plumbing sheets and the kitchen equipment schedule. Changes get expensive. Moving a trap after the concrete cures means cutting the floor, so we catch problems while it is still paper.

Ghost kitchens with four or five brands under one roof often share a single trap, so the fixture count can be higher than the floor plan suggests. Each brand's prep sink counts.
