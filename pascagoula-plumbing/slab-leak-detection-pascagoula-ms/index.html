    Precise Slab Leak Detection Services in Pascagoula, MS

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Pascagoula

    Our technicians use highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Pascagoula home.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Pascagoula, MS

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Pascagoula

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's particularly effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Pascagoula homes.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Pascagoula

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Leak Detection for Older Mississippi Homes in Pascagoula, MS

    Many older homes in Pascagoula, MS have copper or galvanized steel pipes that are more susceptible to corrosion and leaks over time. Our technicians are highly experienced in detecting leaks in these older systems, understanding their common failure points. We provide careful analysis for the challenges presented by vintage plumbing under concrete slabs.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Pascagoula

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Pascagoula

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs quick attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep some water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Pascagoula

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.
