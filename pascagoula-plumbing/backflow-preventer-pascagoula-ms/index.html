    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer in Pascagoula

    Annual residential backflow testing typically costs $75 to $150 per assembly. Repairs with a rebuild kit usually run $150 to $450. New residential installation ranges from $300 to $1,200, and commercial RPZ assemblies often cost $1,500 to $5,000 installed.

    Water providers may send follow-up notices, charge fees or eventually suspend service. Scheduling early avoids these problems. We can often fit urgent tests in quickly.

    DFM Plumbing services all major types of backflow preventers, including Reduced Pressure Zone Assemblies, Double Check Valve Assemblies, Pressure Vacuum Breakers, and Atmospheric Vacuum Breakers. We work with popular brands like Watts, Wilkins, Febco, and Conbraco.

    The lifespan of a backflow preventer typically ranges from 10 to 20 years, depending on the type, manufacturer, water quality, and maintenance. Regular testing and timely repairs can extend its life. However, certain components like rubber seals may need replacement sooner due to normal wear.

    Backflow preventers stop contaminated water from flowing backward into the public supply during pressure drops. Irrigation, fire lines and commercial equipment are common sources of contamination. Water providers require them to protect public health.

    Signs of a failing backflow preventer include discolored water, strange odors from your tap, a noticeable drop in water pressure, or visible leaks around the device itself. If you notice any of these, contact a professional plumber immediately to assess the issue.
