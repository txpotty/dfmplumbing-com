    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Pascagoula

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Pascagoula

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Pascagoula residence.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Pascagoula

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Pascagoula due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, avoiding electrical hazards and supporting longevity.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Pascagoula

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Pascagoula

    Flash flooding can overwhelm storm drains and back up sewer lines, particularly in older neighborhoods. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Pascagoula

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a regular basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Pascagoula, MS

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.
