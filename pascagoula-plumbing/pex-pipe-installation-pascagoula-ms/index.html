    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Pascagoula

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Pascagoula can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Pascagoula, MS

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Pascagoula

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Pascagoula, MS varies with the scope of the project, such as a full repipe or a small repair. In general, homeowners can expect to pay between $3,000 and $10,000 for a whole-home repipe, while smaller projects might range from $500 to $2,000. We provide detailed, transparent estimates before starting any work.

    We install Uponor PEX-A along with Viega, Zurn and SharkBite PEX-B systems. We recommend the system that fits your project and budget.

    Multiple pinhole leaks, discolored water, low pressure throughout the house and gray polybutylene pipe are common signs. Recurring slab leaks are also a good reason to repipe overhead.

    We install quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other proven brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Pascagoula homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light breaks down it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or fully covered.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is strongly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are essential for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.
