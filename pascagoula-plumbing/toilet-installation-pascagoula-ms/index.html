    While it's possible for a homeowner to attempt a DIY toilet installation, it's often best left to professionals.     A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, typically around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.
