    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Pascagoula, MS

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Pascagoula, MS homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. We check the panel first.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Pascagoula, MS

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Homes around Pascagoula on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a filter. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.
