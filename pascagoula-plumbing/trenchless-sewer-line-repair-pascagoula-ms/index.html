    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Pascagoula

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Pascagoula homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Pascagoula

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Pascagoula

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Pascagoula that require full replacement rather than just relining.
