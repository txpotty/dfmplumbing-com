    Why Polybutylene Fails in Pascagoula

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Homes around Pascagoula on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a treatment system. Hot water speeds it up.
