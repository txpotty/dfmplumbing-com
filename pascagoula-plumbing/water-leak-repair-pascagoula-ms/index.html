    Leaks inside walls show up as bubbling paint, soft drywall, baseboard stains or a musty smell. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find the wet area and the leaking pipe.     Fixture and Supply Line Leak Repair in Pascagoula, MS

    Many leaks are at faucets, toilets, angle stops and braided supply lines. These small drips can rot cabinet floors and waste hundreds of gallons.     Hidden Leak Detection in Pascagoula

    Many leaks occur out of sight, within walls, under floors, or behind appliances, leading to serious damage before they become visible.     Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Pascagoula

    Slab leaks are frequent where soil shifts and puts stress on pipes embedded in concrete foundations. Our repair options range from targeted slab penetration to re-routing the affected line, minimizing disruption and protecting your foundation from further damage. We work with all types of piping found in slab foundations, including copper and PEX, delivering a durable fix that complies with local building codes.
