    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Pascagoula

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Pascagoula, MS

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Sewer gas follows soon after. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, which is why we look at the vent whenever the lateral checks out fine. Fixes range from a quick clearing to a new section of stack.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Pascagoula, MS

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Pascagoula

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.
