    PEX Repiping in Pascagoula

    PEX repipes go faster than copper. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Pascagoula

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Iron can leave rusty stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Pascagoula, MS

    One leak on otherwise good pipe is a repair. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it tells us the system is failing everywhere, and patching just moves the next leak down the line. We show you what we find.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Pascagoula, MS

    Wrapping exposed lines and letting faucets drip on the coldest nights prevent most of the damage a sudden freeze can cause. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, and when it bursts we replace the damaged section with PEX or copper and add insulation or reroute it away from the cold. Then we test it.
