    PEX Water Line Installation in Pascagoula, MS

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and increasingly popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it very durable for most water conditions. Freezing weather is infrequent in Mississippi, but a hard freeze every few winters can catch homes that were never built for it. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a real advantage when a cold snap hits Pascagoula. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and faster installation.
