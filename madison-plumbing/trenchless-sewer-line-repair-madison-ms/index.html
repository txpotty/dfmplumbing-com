    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Madison, MS

    Sewer lines in Madison, MS can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.
