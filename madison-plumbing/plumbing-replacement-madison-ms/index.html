    Water Heater Replacement in Madison

    Water heaters in Madison that leak from the tank, rumble on every heat cycle or run out of hot water halfway through a shower are usually cheaper to replace than to keep repairing. A leak from the tank body itself cannot be fixed. We match the fuel and size, update the venting and add an expansion tank or drain pan if code calls for one. Hot water is back by evening.

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Madison, MS

    A dead sump pump on a wet night means water on the floor, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Madison, MS, we replace sump pumps with cast iron or thermoplastic models sized for your inflow, add a check valve, and recommend a battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and the pressure switch setting. Both get tested before we go.

    Well Pump and Pressure Tank Replacement in Madison, MS

    Rural properties near Madison, MS depend on the well pump for every drop, so a failure is an emergency. Common signs include pressure that rises and falls, sputtering faucets, a pump that runs constantly or a breaker that keeps tripping, and any of those means the pump, switch or tank is close to giving out. Pumps rarely recover once this starts.

    Well Pump & Pressure Tank Replacement in Madison

    Rural homes around Madison that run on a well have no water at all when the pump quits. We pull the old submersible pump, check the wire and drop pipe for damage, set the new pump at the right depth and test flow and pressure, often replacing the pressure tank and switch at the same visit. Most jobs finish same day.

    Water Line Replacement in Madison, MS

    A leaking water service line runs your meter all day and night and can soften the soil next to the foundation. In Madison, MS, we replace old galvanized, polybutylene and failing copper service lines with PEX or copper from the meter to the house and add a new full-port main shutoff at the foundation wall. One day covers most.

    Gas Line Replacement in Madison

    Old black iron or corroded gas lines in Madison homes can leak at threaded joints, and a gas leak is not something to wait on. If you smell gas, leave the house, then call the gas utility from outside, and once the line is safe, we replace the damaged section or the whole run, pressure test it and relight every appliance. We test every joint first.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Madison

    A well pump that clicks on and off constantly usually points to a failed pressure tank. When the bladder inside the tank ruptures, the tank fills with water, loses its air cushion and forces the pump to start every time someone opens a tap, which burns out pump motors years early. A new tank fixes it. The pressure switch gets checked too.

    Water Softener Replacement in Madison, MS

    Softeners wear out as the resin breaks down and the control valve stops cycling properly. Iron can leave rusty stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Once scale returns, dishes spot or salt stops dropping, we test hardness, size a new unit and plumb it with a bypass so the house keeps water during service. New valves use less salt.
