    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Madison, MS Home

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Madison

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Madison, MS

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Madison, MS

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Madison

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. New flex connectors every time. We soap-test the gas joints and check the draft hood with the burner lit.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Madison

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Madison, MS

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. Those burners need more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Madison

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. Sizing and gas hookup are part of the job. We confirm the vent meets their specs.

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.
