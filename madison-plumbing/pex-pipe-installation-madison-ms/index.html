    Copper to PEX Conversions in Madison

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Homes around Madison on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a treatment system. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow.     PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Madison, MS

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.
