    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Madison, MS: Style by Style

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Madison

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Madison, MS

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Madison

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Madison

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Madison, MS

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Madison

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Madison

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. It is often the quickest swap. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Madison, MS

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out needs replacing, not patching. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, which makes same-day replacement common, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. We swap the old stops as well.
