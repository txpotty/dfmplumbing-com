    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Madison

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Madison, MS

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Madison, MS. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to enhance separation efficiency. We provide professional installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for optimal performance and automated FOG removal.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Madison, MS

    Correct sizing and smart placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an avoidable expense. Our plumbers carefully calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, delivering optimal performance for your Madison, MS business.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Madison

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. DFM Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Madison for maximum efficiency.
