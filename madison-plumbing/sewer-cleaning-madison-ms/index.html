    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Cleaning in Madison

    The cost of sewer cleaning in Madison can range from $250 to $700, depending on the severity of the clog, the method used, such as snaking or hydro jetting, and whether a camera inspection is required.     Routine sewer cleaning usually does not require a permit in Madison, MS. However, if the cleaning reveals significant damage that requires excavation or major repair work, then permits may be necessary. Our team will tell you about any permit requirements.

    We use professional equipment from trusted brands like Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz for our sewer cleaning work. These brands are known for their durability and effectiveness in tackling tough blockages.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to thoroughly clean the inside of your sewer pipes, removing grease, sludge, and roots. It is used for stubborn blockages, preventative maintenance, or when traditional snaking is not enough to restore full pipe diameter.

    Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover routine sewer cleaning or damage from gradual wear and tear. However, if a sudden, unexpected event causes sewer damage, it might be covered. It is best to check with your insurance provider first.
