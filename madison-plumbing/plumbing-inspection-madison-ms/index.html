    Leak Detection and Assessment in Madison, MS

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Early detection limits the damage. Low, wet ground in many areas also means poor drainage, which matters for sewer lines and septic fields alike.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Madison, MS

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Madison, MS

    Constant moisture accelerates corrosion on metal pipes and fittings and encourages mold around slow leaks. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. We look at pipe insulation. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Madison, MS

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Madison

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. Our pre-purchase inspection covers the supply pipe type, the sewer condition on video, the water heater's age and every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Water Heater Inspections in Madison

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Madison, MS

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Madison

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. Expansion tanks, drain pans and softeners get a look as well, and a quick hardness test tells us whether the softener is actually doing its job or just using salt.
