    Metered Softener Replacement in Madison

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Madison

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Madison. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Madison, MS

    For homeowners in Madison, MS seeking an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach.
