    Picking the Right Softener for Madison, MS Homes

    Metered Softener Replacement in Madison

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Madison

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Madison, MS

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. We set it before we leave.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Madison

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Madison

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Madison, MS

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Madison

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. Sizing comes from peak flow and daily use. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Madison, MS

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. A basic 32,000-grain metered unit costs far less than a twin-tank Kinetico, and for a small household on moderately hard water it may be all you need. We show both options side by side.
