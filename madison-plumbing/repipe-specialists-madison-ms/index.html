    Repiping for Hard Water in Madison, MS

    Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Madison, MS

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.
