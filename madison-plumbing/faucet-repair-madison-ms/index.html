    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Madison

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. Anything worn gets replaced, and the rest gets checked. Compression, cartridge, ball and disc faucets all fail differently, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Madison

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Madison, MS

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. Each brand uses its own part number. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Madison

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. It happens faster with hard water. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Madison

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Exposed pipes in crawlspaces, outdoor spigots and well equipment are the first to fail during a rare deep cold snap. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Madison

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Madison, MS

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Madison, MS

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. We bring the common ones. Filtered water faucets at the kitchen sink get new cartridges or check valves as needed.
