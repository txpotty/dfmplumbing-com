    To extend your septic system's life, keep up regular pumping every 3 to 5 years, avoid flushing non-biodegradable items, minimize harsh chemical use, conserve water and keep vehicles off the drain field. Regular inspections by DFM Plumbing can also catch small issues before they become bigger problems.

    Septic tank repairs are complex and can be dangerous, involving exposure to raw sewage and potential soil contamination. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional like DFM Plumbing so the repair is done safely, effectively and in compliance with local health codes.

    Common causes of septic tank failure include infrequent pumping, which lets solids accumulate, tree root intrusion into pipes or the tank, excessive water usage overloading the system, driving heavy vehicles over the drain field and using harsh chemicals that kill beneficial bacteria.     Most additives are not needed for a healthy system. Regular pumping, water conservation and keeping grease and wipes out of drains do much more.
