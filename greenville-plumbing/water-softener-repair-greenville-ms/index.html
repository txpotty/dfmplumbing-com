    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Greenville, MS

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Fill the tank halfway.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Greenville

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. We test each part. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Resin Bed Problems in Greenville

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Greenville

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Urgent Water Softener Repair in Greenville, MS

    Major leaks and total system failure call for a fast response. DFM Plumbing handles urgent softener calls in Greenville, MS ahead of scheduled maintenance. The unit goes into bypass so you keep water, and then we fix whatever failed. Bypass first.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Greenville, MS

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. DFM Plumbing repairs these units in Greenville, MS. Media lasts a few years.
