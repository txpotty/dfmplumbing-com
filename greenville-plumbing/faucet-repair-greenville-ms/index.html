    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Greenville, MS

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it hard to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break.     Cartridge Faucet Repair in Greenville, MS

    Many modern single-handle faucets, often found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Greenville

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a common choice in many contemporary Greenville bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to stop leaks and restore full performance.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Greenville, MS

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water stains.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Greenville

    Weak or sputtering flow is often caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-house water pressure.
