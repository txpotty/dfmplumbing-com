    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Greenville, MS

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Greenville, MS

    Before any replacement begins, an accurate diagnosis is critical. We use advanced sewer camera inspections to get a clear, real-time view of your underground pipes. This allows us to identify the exact location and nature of the damage, whether it's a crack, collapse, severe blockage, or root intrusion. This diagnostic precision ensures that our sewer replacement plan is tailored specifically to your home's needs in Greenville, MS, avoiding unnecessary work and ensuring a lasting solution.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Greenville, MS

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Greenville, MS

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the full extent of damage. Replacement ends recurring root problems.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Greenville, MS

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging. Methods like pipe bursting or pipe lining allow us to replace or repair your sewer line with minimal excavation, often only requiring two access points. This can save your lawn, driveway, and landscaping from extensive damage, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Greenville who want to preserve their property's appearance. We'll determine if your sewer line is a good candidate for this advanced technique.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Greenville

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most reliable solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often necessary for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure careful planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Greenville

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup snags debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.
