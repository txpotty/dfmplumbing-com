    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Greenville, MS

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Fittings in the box loosen with ground movement and traffic. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, since the utility is in charge of the meter and the pipe feeding it. When it is on your side, we repair it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Greenville

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. With PEX, the fitting is the weak point most of the time. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Greenville, MS

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Repeated leaks on galvanized pipe call for a new line. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. You see the cut-out section yourself.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Greenville, MS

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. Once one spot leaks, the pipe on either side is usually just as thin. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, which can hold for a while if the pipe is not too far gone. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. You get your flow back as a bonus.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Greenville, MS

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patching that kind of pipe tends to buy months, not years. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, typically finishing in one day whether we trench or pull the pipe. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.
