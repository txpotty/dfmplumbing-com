    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Greenville

    Some homes from the 1980s and 1990s may have polybutylene supply pipe, which is known for sudden leaks and is usually replaced. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. Two-person crew, always.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Greenville, MS

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Greenville, MS

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Greenville, MS homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Greenville, MS

We schedule around your hours.
