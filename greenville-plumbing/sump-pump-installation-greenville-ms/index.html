    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Greenville

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding. We install robust, electrically powered pumps designed to automatically activate when water levels rise in the sump pit. Our plumbers will assess your basement's water intrusion risks, considering local water tables and soil types in Greenville, to recommend the right pump size and horsepower for effective water removal.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Greenville

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, running quietly and taking up less space. They are typically more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners. We handle the complete installation of submersible units, including proper sealing and electrical connections, tailored to the particular needs of your Greenville property.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Greenville, MS

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is tight. Our team is experienced in installing these pumps, making sure of proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Greenville, MS.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Greenville

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Greenville, MS

    A pump replacement in an existing pit usually costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the cost but provide extra protection. We give you a written quote that explains each option. That way you can choose the right level of protection for your budget.
