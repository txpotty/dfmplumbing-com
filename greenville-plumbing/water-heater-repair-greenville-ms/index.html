    Electric Water Heater Repair in Greenville

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect.     Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Greenville

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom. We drain the tank, break up scale with repeated fills and check the drain valve for blockage. Flushing restores capacity. On hard water, plan on one flush a year.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Greenville

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. We check each element with an ohmmeter. Readings tell us whether an element is open or shorted, which explains the breaker trips. Common elements and thermostats are stocked.

    Emergency Water Heater Repair in Greenville, MS

    Water heater emergencies happen at all hours. We treat a tank flooding a finished floor or a relief valve that keeps blowing off as a priority call. DFM Plumbing runs emergency repair in Greenville, MS around the clock. Shut the cold supply valve first.

    Pilot Repair in Greenville

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Gas Burner and Venting Repair in Greenville, MS

    Combustion is the part of a gas heater that matters most for safety. Yellow flame tips or soot at the draft hood mean the burner is not getting enough air to burn cleanly. We clean the burner and check draft with a mirror or a smoke test. Backdrafting is dangerous.
