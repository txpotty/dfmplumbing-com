    Commercial Disposal Installation in Greenville

    Commercial disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and typically come with a control panel and solenoid valve and need their own circuit. We set the unit on the mounting collar, run the drain to the grease trap where the local rules require it, and wire the controls so the water runs whenever the motor does. Health codes vary.     Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Greenville

    These disposals run only with the stopper lid seated, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Greenville

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Greenville, MS

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Greenville, MS

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. We haul the old unit away.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Greenville, MS

    If your old disposal quits with a sink full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP models, mounting hardware and cord kits, so we do not need a second trip to the supply house before the sink drains again. Most swaps take about an hour.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Greenville

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. The mount goes on, the unit gets hung and the discharge tube is connected to a new trap, with the wall stub lowered if it sits too high. Power comes from a licensed electrician when needed.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Greenville

    The stopper lid acts as the switch on a batch-feed unit. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. It is the safety feature. Families with kids and anyone nervous about a hand near the hopper tend to choose these in Greenville homes.
