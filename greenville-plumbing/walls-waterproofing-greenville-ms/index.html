    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Greenville

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Greenville, MS can vary a great deal based on the method used, the extent of the damage, and the size of your foundation. Basic crack sealing might range from $300 to $800 per crack, while a full exterior foundation waterproofing project could be $8,000 to $20,000 or more. Interior drainage systems with a sump pump typically fall between $4,000 and $12,000. We provide clear, itemized quotes after a thorough inspection.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies a lot. Smaller jobs like interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while extensive exterior waterproofing with excavation and drainage system installation could take several days to over a week. We'll provide a clear timeline during your first visit.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, usually managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the critical importance of a sound foundation. It often involves heavy excavation, specialized equipment, precise material application, and knowledge of drainage principles and local building codes. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is the smart choice.

    A shower wall waterproofing project typically takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take several days.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Greenville may be required. Simple crack repairs usually do not need a permit. DFM Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and assist with the application process if needed.

    Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also suggest water is getting behind.

    Shower remodels that change plumbing or drain locations often require a permit. Simple membrane replacement may not. We check local requirements and obtain permits when needed.
