    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Greenville

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Greenville, MS can range from $150 to $800, depending on the complexity of the issue and the parts required. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might be on the lower end, while a gas control valve replacement could be higher. We provide a detailed estimate after finding the problem.

    Watch for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.

    Gas tank water heaters typically last 8 to 12 years. Hard water sediment and skipped maintenance can shorten that.

    Most common gas water heater repairs, such as replacing a thermocouple or thermostat, can be finished within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues or those requiring special parts might take longer, but we always aim for same-day repair whenever possible.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't sense heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a very common reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a vital safety component.

    Common causes include a weak thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube, a clogged flame arrestor or poor combustion air. Drafts in the garage can also snuff out older pilots.

    Following the label instructions to relight a pilot is usually safe. If the pilot will not stay lit, or you smell gas, stop and call a licensed plumber.

    In most cases, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit. If the repair involves major changes to the gas line or venting system, or if the unit needs to be replaced, a permit from the Greenville building department might be necessary. We handle all required permitting.
