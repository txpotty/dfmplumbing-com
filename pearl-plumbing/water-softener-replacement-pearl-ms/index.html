    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Pearl

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Pearl

    Restaurants, salons and small businesses rely on soft water to protect dishwashers, espresso machines and water heaters. We replace commercial softeners sized to flow rate and daily usage. Larger valves and multiple tanks may be needed to meet demand. We schedule installs to minimize downtime. We can also add filtration ahead of the softener if needed.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Pearl

    Clack valves are a popular choice for their reliability and customizable settings. If you have an older Clack system failing, DFM Plumbing offers expert Clack water softener replacement in Pearl. We can replace just the control valve or the entire system, ensuring compatibility with your existing setup and configuring the new unit for peak performance.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Pearl

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.
