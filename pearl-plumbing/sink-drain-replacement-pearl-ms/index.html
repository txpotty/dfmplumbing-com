    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Pearl

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The outlet on the disposal has to sit above the trap inlet, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or an air gap, and the trap has to line up without the slip joints pulling sideways. We set them all at once. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Homes around Pearl on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a filter. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes.     Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Pearl

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Pearl

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Pearl, MS

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. One new pop-up fixes every part of that. Takes about an hour.
