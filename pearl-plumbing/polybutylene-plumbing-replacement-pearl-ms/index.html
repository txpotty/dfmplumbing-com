    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Pearl

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. If the meter's leak dial moves with everything off, we track the leak down with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging instead of guessing.     Copper Pipe Replacement in Pearl

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. It costs more. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Pearl, MS

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance, and most homes are completed in two to four working days with water back on each night.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Pearl, MS

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. Buried poly service lines split, run up bills and saturate the yard. We call 811 first. Then we trench the route, install new PEX or copper service pipe and connect it to a new main shutoff at the house.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Pearl

Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.
