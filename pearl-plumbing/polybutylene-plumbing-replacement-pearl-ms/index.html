    Detecting Polybutylene Leaks in Pearl

    Even small, hidden leaks from polybutylene pipes can cause serious damage to your Pearl home's foundation or interior. Our technicians use advanced leak detection equipment, including acoustic sensors and thermal imaging, to pinpoint the exact location of leaks without destructive guesswork. We can identify leaks in walls, under slabs, and behind appliances, providing an accurate assessment before any replacement work begins. Early detection is key to preventing widespread water damage and costly repairs in Mississippi homes.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Pearl

    For homeowners who prefer a traditional, sturdy solution, copper piping is an excellent alternative to polybutylene. Copper is highly durable, resistant to bacteria growth, and has a proven track record of longevity. While typically a higher investment than PEX, copper offers superior strength and reliability. We provide careful copper pipe installation services across Pearl.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Pearl, MS

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance. Most homes are completed in two to four days.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Pearl, MS

    Many poly-era homes also have blue or black polybutylene service lines running from the water meter to the house. These buried lines can split and leak under your yard, raising your bill and soaking the soil. We call 811 to mark utilities, trench the route and install new PEX or copper service pipe. The new line connects to a new main shutoff at the house.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Pearl

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process. Our team specializes in full home repiping using PEX (cross-linked polyethylene), a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant material. PEX is an strong choice for Mississippi homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to handle temperature changes.     Chlorine and chloramine in treated water break down the plastic from the inside, making it brittle. Fittings and hot water lines often fail first.
