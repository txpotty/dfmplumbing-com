    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Pearl

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements.     Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Pearl, MS

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company. It is also the stretch that takes the most weather, sitting in the sun or the soil. Risers rust at grade. We replace the riser or the buried run, pressure test it and leave the meter connection ready for the utility to open the meter. Expect a half day for most riser swap.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Pearl

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test.     Appliance Gas Line Repair in Pearl, MS

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Every time somebody slides the stove out to mop, the connector takes another bend. Valves weep at the stem. The fix is usually a new coated connector and a new ball valve, plus a drip leg if the appliance lacks one. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is lit again.

    Gas Leak Detection in Pearl

    Some leaks announce themselves with a hiss you can hear across the room, and others lose a few cubic feet a day and only show up on the bill. Slow leaks are what the test gauge is for. We isolate, then sniff. We run the test twice, with appliance valves closed and then open, because some leaks only show downstream of a valve. Finding the exact joint keeps the repair small.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Pearl

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. The outdoor branch gets isolated and tested by itself, then the bad run is replaced with polyethylene and a tracer. Above-ground stub-ups get a new riser and a shutoff valve.
