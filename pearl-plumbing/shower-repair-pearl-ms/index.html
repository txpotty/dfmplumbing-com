    Shower Repairs We Handle in Pearl, MS

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Pearl

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. The parts are on the truck. Most valves we open take a Moen, Delta, Kohler or Pfister cartridge, which means a same-visit fix.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Pearl

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. Soaking helps sometimes. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Pearl, MS

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Pearl

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. A new cartridge fixes it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Pearl

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. If the valve body is pitted or parts no longer exist, we swap the valve, often through an access opening from the closet side. The new valve will have scald protection. Thermostatic valves are an option if you want exact temperature control.

    Commercial Shower Service in Pearl

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. With commercial cartridges on board, we fix rooms one at a time so the rest stay open. After-hours work is available. Each repaired valve gets its limit stop set and tested before we move on.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Pearl, MS

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Pearl, MS

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Then call us. DFM Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.
