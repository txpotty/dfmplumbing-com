    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Pearl, MS

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Pearl

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. We schedule around your hours. Night installs are available.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Pearl, MS

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Pearl

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. All work follows local code.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Pearl, MS

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Tankless jobs cost more mostly because of gas line and vent work, while attic installs, moves and code updates add labor to any type. Permits are included. You also get an estimate of yearly energy cost for each option.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Pearl

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Big families do well with gas.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Pearl, MS

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Then we check for leaks. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Pearl, MS

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. Upfront cost is higher. We check gas capacity before quoting.
