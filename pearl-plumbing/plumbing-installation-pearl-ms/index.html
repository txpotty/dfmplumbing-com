    Water Line Installation and Replacement in Pearl

    Sump Pump Installation in Pearl, MS

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. We dig the pit to the right depth, set a primary pump sized to the inflow and add a battery backup if you want one. Where the water exits matters most. The discharge gets carried well away from the foundation so the same water is not pumped twice.

    Water Heater Replacement in Pearl

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment.     Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Pearl, MS

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Pearl

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. One moves groundwater, the other moves sewage, and they are not interchangeable. Sizing comes first. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Pearl, MS

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. Homes with a PRV usually need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Pearl

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. A PEX repipe ends that cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.
