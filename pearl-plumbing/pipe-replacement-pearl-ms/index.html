    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Pearl, MS

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Pearl

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Pearl, MS

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, so we avoid tearing open walls just to pull out dead pipe. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Pearl

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Pearl

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, and keep water on at night whenever we can. Then patching.

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first.
