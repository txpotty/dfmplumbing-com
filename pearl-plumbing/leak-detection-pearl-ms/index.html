    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. Look for warm tile first.     Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Pearl, MS

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Pearl, MS

    A silent toilet leak can lose hundreds of gallons a day. Worn faucet cartridges, softener valves and water heater connectors add more.
