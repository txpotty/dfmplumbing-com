    Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Plumbing in Pearl

    The cost of new construction plumbing in Pearl varies a lot based on the size and complexity of the project, number of bathrooms, and fixture choices. For a standard 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home, rough-in and finish plumbing can range from $10,000 to $25,000. Commercial projects or luxury homes with advanced systems will be higher.

    Some jurisdictions allow owner-builders to do their own plumbing on a home they will live in, but the work still has to pass every inspection. Under-slab and gas work are the hardest to get right and the most expensive to fix later. Many owner-builders hire us for those phases and finish easier tasks themselves.

    Slow drains on a brand new home, gurgling fixtures, sewer odors or a stub-out that ended up under a wall are typical red flags. These usually trace back to poor slope, missing vents or layout errors before the pour. A camera inspection can confirm the cause before the problem grows.

    For new construction, we usually use PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, for water supply lines due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion. For drain, waste, and vent systems, we primarily use PVC piping because it's strong, lightweight, and cost-effective.

    Water heaters in hot garages and attics wear faster, so their condition is worth checking during any plumbing visit. Wrapping exposed lines and letting faucets drip on the coldest nights avoid most of the damage a sudden freeze can cause. We select materials and installation methods that can withstand local conditions, insulating pipes and burying lines at adequate depths where freezing is a risk. We also use materials that can handle thermal expansion and contraction, helping prevent leaks and bursts.

    The timeline for new construction plumbing depends on the project's scope. The rough-in phase, which involves installing the hidden pipes, typically takes 1 to 2 weeks. The final fixture installation and connection usually takes another 3 to 5 days, spread out during the later stages of construction. We work closely with your general contractor to stay on schedule.
