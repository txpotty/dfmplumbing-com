    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Pearl, MS

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see.     Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Pearl, MS

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. The carrier, 2 inch waste, 3/4 inch supply and vent all go in to the spec sheet before drywall. Inspectors see it open. Then the walls close.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Pearl

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Models like the Washbrook and the Bardon come in versions from 0.125 to 1.0 gallons per flush, and each one needs a flush valve matched to that rating or the bowl will either splash or not rinse clean. Matching is simple with the spec sheet. We order both together.

    Office and Restaurant Urinal Installation in Pearl

    Restaurant restrooms see a lot of traffic in short bursts. Busy bars can see hundreds of flushes a night, so we install valves that recover fast and carriers that stay tight. We can fit tamper-resistant valves. Night installs keep you open.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Pearl

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. The old flush valve comes off, the supply gets capped behind the wall, and the new waterless bowl goes on the same carrier with its cartridge housing tied into the existing drain. Many jurisdictions want a capped supply kept for later. That allows a future change back.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Pearl

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Pearl, MS

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Pearl, MS

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. Permits are on us. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.
