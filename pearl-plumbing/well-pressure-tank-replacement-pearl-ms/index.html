    Replacing Failed Well Tanks in Pearl, MS

    DFM Plumbing replaces well pressure tanks, the steel tank that holds water under air pressure between the pump and your faucets. A rubber bladder inside keeps the air charge apart from the water. Once that bladder tears, the tank fills with water, the air cushion disappears, and the pump begins short cycling. Pumps hate that.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Pearl, MS

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Pearl

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Pearl

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, since many original tanks were undersized from the start. Check before swapping.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Pearl, MS

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. Common sizes are easy for us to get. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, then confirming the gauge, switch and drawdown line up with the pump you have. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Pearl, MS

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. It is one of the most common tanks we install. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, which helps the tank resist rust from the inside, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.
