    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue.     Leaky Pipe Repair in Pearl, MS

    Small leaks raise water bills and grow mold in hidden spaces. Check the meter. If the dial moves with every fixture off, there is a leak somewhere, so we shut off branches one at a time to find which part of the system is losing water. Then we open one spot.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Pearl, MS

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Pearl

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, which is why we use dielectric transitions and lay out a replacement plan for the rest. The schedule is yours.
