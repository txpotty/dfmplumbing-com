    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and stands up to roots.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Pearl, MS

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. DFM Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Pearl, MS

    A broken water service line wastes water and soaks the yard. Pressure falls too. We locate the break, then replace the line by bursting new HDPE through the old path or boring a new path, so the lawn and walkway stay put and you are back in water quickly. The new line gets pressure tested.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Pearl

    Commercial properties have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Tearing it out costs plenty.     Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Pearl

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Pearl, MS

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Pearl, MS soil.

    Sectional Point Repair in Pearl

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Pearl sewer lines.
