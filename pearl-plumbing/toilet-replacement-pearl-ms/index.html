    Common Toilet Replacements in Pearl, MS

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Pearl

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Pearl

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Pearl, MS

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Pearl, MS

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. You get repair and replacement prices next to each other. The decision is yours, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Pearl, MS

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Pearl, MS

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Pearl, MS

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Pearl

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. It needs to be replaced. We pull the cracked toilet and check the floor for water damage before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.
