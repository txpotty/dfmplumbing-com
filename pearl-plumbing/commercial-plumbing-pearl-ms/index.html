    Commercial Plumbing Services We Handle in Pearl, MS

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Pearl

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Expansive clay soils are common in Mississippi, swelling with rain and shrinking in dry spells, which puts stress on foundations and buried pipes. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. We price both so you can compare.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Pearl, MS

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. We repair broken baffles, replace cracked lids and outlet tees, jet the inlet lines and confirm the unit is sized for your fixture count and flow rate. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. Keeping the pumping log current helps at inspection time.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Pearl

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers. We also handle ADA upgrades. For ADA remodels we set fixture heights, add grab bar blocking in the wall and insulate the drain and supply lines under sinks.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Pearl

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Pearl

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. The video is yours to keep. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Pearl

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. A sectional machine opens the line, and a jetter cleans grease off the walls of the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so it stays open longer. We also check trap primers.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Pearl, MS

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. We install and service high-input tank heaters, tankless racks and boiler-fed indirect systems with storage tanks. Iron can leave orange stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Mineral scale chokes output quickly. We set up a flushing and descaling schedule, and we install thermostatic mixing valves so hand sinks deliver tempered water without scalding anyone.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Pearl, MS

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless racks get descaled. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.
