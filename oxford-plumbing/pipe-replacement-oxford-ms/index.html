    PEX Repiping in Oxford

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Oxford

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Oxford

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a common choice for modern pipe replacement projects in Oxford due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing. It's also less expensive to install than copper. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. DFM Plumbing expertly installs PEX piping for full home repipes or targeted section replacements, providing a reliable and long-lasting plumbing solution that handles local water conditions well.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Oxford, MS

    Many older homes in Oxford, MS still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to rust and corrosion on the inside. This leads to reduced water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. DFM Plumbing provides complete galvanized pipe replacement services, removing these failing pipes and installing new, modern materials. Upgrading from galvanized pipes not only improves your water quality and pressure but also shields your home from potential water damage caused by deteriorating plumbing.

    Burst Pipe Repair & Replacement in Oxford, MS

    Freezing weather is infrequent in Mississippi, but a hard freeze every few winters can catch homes that were never built for it. Cold snaps can lead to burst pipes, especially in exposed areas or unheated spaces. When a pipe bursts in your Oxford, MS home, it causes immediate water damage and requires urgent attention. Our team provides emergency service to shut off water, remove damaged sections, and replace them with new, more freeze-resistant piping, often PEX, to prevent future occurrences. We work fast to minimize disruption and water loss.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Oxford

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be damaging and difficult to detect. These leaks often stem from corroded or damaged pipes embedded within or under your home's slab. DFM Plumbing uses advanced leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, avoiding future issues in the same spot.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Oxford, MS

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace accessible sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage in hand.
