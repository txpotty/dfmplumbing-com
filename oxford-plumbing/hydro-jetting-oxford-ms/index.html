    Hydro jetting is exceptionally effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often misses.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and flush debris away.

    For most homes in Oxford, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes.     Usually, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines.
