    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Oxford, MS

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Low-flow and waterless units leave mineral buildup in the line, so proper slope and a cleanout matter. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Oxford

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handles can go left or right. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Oxford, MS

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Oxford, MS

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Oxford, MS

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. Each Sloan Royal gets the right inside parts for the bowl, a tuned control stop and a rebuild kit on hand. Sensor versions mount the same way. Battery models need no wiring.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Oxford, MS

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Unheated garages need freeze planning. Ask us about the layout.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Oxford

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Oxford, MS

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.
