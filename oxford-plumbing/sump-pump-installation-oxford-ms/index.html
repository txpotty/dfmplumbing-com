    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Oxford, MS

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. Our team is skilled in installing these pumps, making sure of proper pit depth and discharge line setup for reliable performance in Oxford, MS.

    Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Oxford, MS

    Severe storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out.
