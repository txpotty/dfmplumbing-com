    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Oxford, MS

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Oxford, MS, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Oxford

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Oxford home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Oxford, MS

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more efficiently and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Oxford, MS, where mineral content in the water is a frequent concern.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Oxford

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.     Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Oxford, MS

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can ruin a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Oxford, MS

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.
