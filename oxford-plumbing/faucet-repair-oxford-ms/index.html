    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Oxford, MS

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Oxford

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Oxford, MS

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Oxford, MS

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Oxford, MS

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Oxford, MS

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature.     Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Oxford, MS

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor.
