    Well Pump Control Box & Electrical Repair in Oxford, MS

    Many well pump failures stem from electrical issues within the control box or wiring. This can include tripped breakers, faulty capacitors, or corroded connections.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Oxford

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Oxford, MS

    Accurate diagnosis is the first step to an effective well pump repair.     Control Box and Electrical Repair in Oxford, MS

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Flash flooding can overwhelm storm drains and back up sewer lines, particularly in older neighborhoods. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Oxford

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Oxford.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Oxford, MS

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive typically shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but correct setup.
