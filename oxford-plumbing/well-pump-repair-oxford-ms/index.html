    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Oxford

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. Testing starts at the surface. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Submersible Pump Pulling and Repair in Oxford

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. The motor, the wire splice and the check valve are the other common failures. We test the motor and wire from the surface before deciding whether to pull. New pumps match your well's yield.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Oxford

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Oxford, MS well.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Oxford

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Oxford, MS

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. High amp draw points to a weak motor. A clogged intake or a dropping water level shows up as low flow with normal amps, and that changes the repair entirely. Testing first saves a pull.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Oxford

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Oxford, MS

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Oxford

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.
