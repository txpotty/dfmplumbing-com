    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Oxford

    The cost for a gas water heater installation in Oxford typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,000, depending on the unit size, type (tank vs. tankless) and any needed gas line or venting modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after a site assessment.

    Power vent units use a fan to push exhaust outside, while direct vent units use a sealed system that draws outside air. Both allow more flexible placement compared to atmospheric vents.

    The main difference is the type of gas they use and the burners and orifices required for combustion. Natural gas is typically supplied by a utility line, while propane is stored in a tank. We install both types and confirm correct setup for your fuel source.

    A conventional gas water heater stores and heats a large volume of water in a tank, providing a limited supply. A gas tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit, offering an endless supply but requiring more involved venting and gas line work.

    Rusty water, leaks, pilot problems and slow recovery are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement may be the smarter option.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment and checking the anode rod, can significantly extend your gas water heater's lifespan. Our team can advise on best practices and offer maintenance services.

    Most new gas water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components. DFM Plumbing also provides a warranty on our installation labor. We will explain both during your consultation.

    In most areas, a permit is generally required for gas water heater installation to confirm compliance with local building and plumbing codes.
