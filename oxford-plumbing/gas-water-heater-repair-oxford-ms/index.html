    Venting and Draft Repair in Oxford, MS

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Oxford

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a malfunctioning igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to ensure a quick fix and restore your hot water supply promptly.

    Thermostat and Gas Valve Repair in Oxford, MS

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is overheating, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame. These components regulate the gas supply and temperature. Our plumbers in Oxford, MS can test the thermostat for accuracy and inspect the gas valve for any blockages or malfunctions. We carry a range of replacement parts for various brands, allowing for quick and efficient repairs.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Oxford

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane rather than natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Oxford, MS

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.
