    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Oxford, MS

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Older homes in Mississippi often have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains, both of which corrode and restrict flow with age. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Oxford

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that picks up the cooler wet area, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Oxford

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Oxford

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Low, wet ground in many areas also means poor drainage, which matters for sewer lines and septic fields alike. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Oxford, MS

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.
