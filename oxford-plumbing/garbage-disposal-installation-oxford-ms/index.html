    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Oxford, MS

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Oxford

    These disposals run only with the stopper lid seated, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Oxford

    For kitchens that have never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. We install the mount, hang the disposal and connect the discharge tube to a new trap, lowering the drain at the wall if the old stub sits too high for the disposal outlet. An electrician adds power if needed.

    Garbage Disposal Installation Cost Factors in Oxford, MS

    The model and the power setup drive most of the cost. A plain swap with power already in place costs the least, and new wiring, a lowered drain or a reworked double bowl each add labor as a separate line. The quote covers unit and labor.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Oxford, MS

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The drain usually stays the same.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Oxford, MS

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. We haul the old unit away.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Oxford, MS

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. Islands use them often.
