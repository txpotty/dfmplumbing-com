    What We Install in Oxford, MS

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Oxford, MS

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. An ejector pump in a sealed basin lifts it up to the main drain. Pump and basin are sized to the fixtures, the basin is vented to code, and the discharge gets a check valve and a shutoff so the pump can come out for service. Brands we trust here are Liberty and Zoeller.

    Softener Loop and Conditioner Installs in Oxford

    Homes around Oxford on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a treatment system. Some homes already have a softener loop plumbed in, usually in the garage, which turns the install into a two-hour job. No loop means we cut into the main line and add one. That adds a few hours. A salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner fits the same loop.

    PEX Repiping in Oxford

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. It also handles a freeze better. Exposed pipes in crawlspaces, outdoor spigots and well equipment are the first to fail during a rare deep cold snap. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.

    Water Line Installation and Replacement in Oxford

    Corroded galvanized and brittle polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. New lines go in as PEX or Type L copper, with crimp or expansion fittings on the plastic and solder on the copper. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. We bed buried pipe in sand at the right depth so stones and settling soil cannot chafe it over time. Above 80 psi, we add a pressure reducing valve.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Oxford, MS

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Gas Line Installation in Oxford, MS

    A new range or an outdoor kitchen all start with the same math: total BTU load and the length of the run. We size the pipe from that, pick black iron or CSST, and trench where the line goes underground. Every joint gets a soap test. After that, the line sits on a pressure gauge until the inspector signs off.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Oxford, MS

    Electric tankless heaters pull serious amperage, often 120 amps or more for a whole-house unit. Before we quote one, we check that your panel and service can carry that load, and we bring in a licensed electrician when they cannot. Plumbing is the easy part. We set the flow rate and outlet temperature so the shower does not run cold when the dishwasher kicks on.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Oxford

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.
