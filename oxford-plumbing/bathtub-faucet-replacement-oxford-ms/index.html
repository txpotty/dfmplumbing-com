    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Oxford, MS

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Oxford

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Oxford, MS

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. Snapped handles and broken spouts get same-day visits when a plumber is free. Shut off the water first. Leave the tub drain open.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Oxford, MS

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Oxford, MS

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A cartridge plus a same-brand trim kit can refresh an old faucet for far less than a new valve. We offer that option first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Oxford

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Oxford, MS

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Oxford, MS

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.
