    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. The lowest fixture backs up first.     Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Oxford, MS

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, and we locate the bad section on camera before replacing it with wrapped perforated pipe in washed stone.     Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Oxford, MS

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Repairs meet local plumbing code.
