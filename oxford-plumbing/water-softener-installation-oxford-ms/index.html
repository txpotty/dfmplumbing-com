    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Oxford

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning.     Pelican Water Softener Installation in Oxford, MS

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Startup matters. We follow it step by step.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Oxford

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Oxford, MS

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Softener Loop Installation in Oxford
