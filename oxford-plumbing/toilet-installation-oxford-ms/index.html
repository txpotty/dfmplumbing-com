    Types of Toilet Installation in Oxford, MS

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Oxford, MS

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. A MaP rating of 800 grams or better is what we look for, since a weak low-flow toilet that needs double flushing saves no water at all. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Oxford

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Oxford, MS

    Bought the toilet yourself? Check the rough-in printed on the box first. Before the old toilet comes out, we inspect the new one for shipping cracks, because cracked china goes back to the store, not onto your flange. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Oxford

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Oxford

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Oxford

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Oxford, MS

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Oxford

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. We keep common two-piece toilets on the truck. In many cases we can pull the broken one and set a replacement in the same visit, with a 12 inch rough-in model in round or elongated. Close the stop until then.
