    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Oxford, MS

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Oxford, MS

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Newer houses and manufactured homes mostly use PEX or CPVC supply lines with PVC drains. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out.     Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Oxford, MS

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.
