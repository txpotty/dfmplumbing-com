    What Goes Into an Ejector Install in Oxford, MS

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Oxford

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Oxford, MS

    Businesses in Oxford, MS with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. A duplex system with an alternating panel is common, so if one pump fails the other keeps things running. Closed restrooms cost business. Each pump can carry the load alone.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Oxford, MS

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A smaller laundry tray pump or a compact ejector can handle it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer dumps at once, with a lint filter so fibers do not wrap the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Oxford, MS

    Upgrading an old ejector does not always mean a new basin. With a sound basin, a new pump, fresh check valve, shutoff and alarm give you most of a new system for far less. We tell you if the basin is done.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Oxford, MS

    The basin holds everything, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. Bigger basins let the pump run longer, fewer times a day, which is easier on the motor and switch, so we size up when the pit serves a laundry or several fixtures. Every lid gets sealed.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Oxford

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. Grinders run pricier. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Oxford, MS

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Redundancy matters here.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Oxford

    Digging the pit starts with a saw cut through the floor, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Low, wet ground in many areas also means slow drainage, which matters for sewer lines and septic fields alike. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.
