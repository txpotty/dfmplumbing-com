    Picking the Right Softener for Oxford, MS Water

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Oxford, MS

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Mississippi homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Oxford, MS

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. We set it before we leave.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Oxford

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Oxford

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Or the whole system.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Oxford, MS

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. For a small house on moderately hard water, a basic metered single-tank unit often does the job for much less than a twin-tank system. We show both options side by side.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Oxford, MS

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. They need no drain line. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Oxford

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Oxford

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. Sizing comes from peak flow and daily use. A carbon prefilter may go first.
