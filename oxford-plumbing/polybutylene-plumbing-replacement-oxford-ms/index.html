    Why Polybutylene Fails in Oxford

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Iron can leave orange stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
