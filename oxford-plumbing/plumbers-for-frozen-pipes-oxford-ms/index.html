    Frozen Pipe Thawing in Oxford

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line somewhere. We open a faucet downstream to relieve pressure, then locate the frozen section with thermal imaging. Copper and galvanized pipes can be thawed with an electric pipe thawing machine that warms the metal from the inside. PEX and CPVC are warmed slowly with heat guns or infrared heaters to avoid softening the plastic.

    Heat Tape Installation for Exposed Pipes in Oxford

    For pipes in particularly cold or exposed areas, standard insulation might not be enough. DFM Plumbing offers professional heat tape installation, an electric heating cable that warms pipes to prevent freezing. We handle proper installation, including thermostats and ground fault circuit interrupters, for safe and effective operation. This solution is ideal for pipes in crawl spaces, well houses, or along exterior walls in Oxford.
