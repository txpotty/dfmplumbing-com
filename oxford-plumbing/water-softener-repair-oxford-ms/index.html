    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Oxford

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener probably needs attention. DFM Plumbing offers complete diagnostic services to identify the root cause of these hard water symptoms. We check water hardness levels, inspect all system components, and provide an accurate assessment and repair plan suited to your home in Oxford.

    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Oxford

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the culprit. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process revitalizes your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Oxford

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Control Valve Repair in Oxford

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. DFM Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, restoring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Oxford.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Oxford, MS

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Oxford

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. DFM Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Oxford.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Oxford, MS

    Leaks around your water softener can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, faulty O-rings, or pressure issues. Even a small leak can lead to water damage and wasted water. Our team uses dedicated tools to quickly locate the source of any leak, whether it's a slow drip or a significant pool. We then perform careful repairs, from tightening connections and replacing seals to patching cracks or replacing damaged components, protecting your home from water intrusion.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Oxford

    A softener using far too much salt or water usually has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can noticeably cut salt and water use.
