    Hydro-Jetting Pre-Inspection in Oxford, MS

    Jetting a cracked or collapsing pipe can make it worse. So we look first. A quick scope in Oxford, MS shows whether the pipe walls are solid enough to handle thousands of psi or whether a gentler cleaning and a repair plan make more sense. It takes about 20 minutes.

    Residential Drain Camera Inspection in Oxford

    Kitchen, bath and laundry lines inside the house get a smaller camera head. Branch lines are tight. We can follow a 1 1/2 inch line through its bends, see where it ties into the main and check whether it has the right slope or a dip where water and grease sit. It explains a lot of slow drains.

    Identifying Specific Pipe Damage in Oxford

    Each kind of damage looks different on camera. Cracks show as dark lines. Offsets show as a lip where one pipe section has dropped below the next, sags show standing water and corroded iron looks rough and flaky. The type of damage decides the fix.

    Identifying Tree Root Intrusion in Oxford, MS

    Roots on camera look like fine hairs at first. Later they fill the pipe. Our footage shows where they come in, how many joints are affected and whether the root mass is a light fringe that cleaning will handle or a dense ball that is already cracking the pipe. That decides the fix. Light roots need cutting, heavy ones need lining or replacement.

    Locating Lost Items with Camera in Oxford, MS

    Rings, earrings and small toys go down drains every day. Most stop in the trap. When they get past it, a small camera can find the item in the branch line and show us whether we can fish it out from the nearest opening or need to open a fitting. Much cheaper than guessing.
