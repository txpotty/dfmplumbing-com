    Sewer Line Repair Jobs We Handle in Oxford, MS

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Oxford, MS

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Oxford, MS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Oxford

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Oxford often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Oxford, MS

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Patching that line is wasted money. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Mississippi property, showing what each method costs and what it does to the yard. The right pick depends on depth and access.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Oxford, MS

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often make it the cheaper total job. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Oxford

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Oxford

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. Daylight digs are safer around buried utilities.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Oxford, MS

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, with no digging at all. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.
