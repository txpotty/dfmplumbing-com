    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Oxford, MS

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to verify flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and settling.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Oxford, MS

    Hydro jetting is a proven solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Oxford, MS. This method uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is very effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Oxford, MS

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for repair or replacement.

    Catch Basin Cleaning in Oxford, MS

    Catch basins are critical parts of any storm drainage system in Oxford, MS, designed to collect runoff and trap debris before it enters the main drain lines. Regular cleaning of catch basins is essential to prevent them from becoming overloaded and causing blockages further down the system. Our service includes removing built-up leaves, sediment, trash, and other debris from catch basins, so they work correctly and protect your entire drainage system. This preventative maintenance is key to avoiding larger issues.
