    Cost depends mostly on run length, soil conditions and pipe material.     Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Oxford, MS

    Many homes built decades ago have a 3/4 inch line that cannot keep up with modern fixture loads, irrigation and pools. If your pressure drops when two showers run or the sprinklers are on, an undersized line may be the culprit. We test static and flowing pressure, then calculate whether a larger line will make a real difference. Upsizing is often paired with a new pressure reducing valve and main shutoff.

    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Oxford, MS

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.
