    Water Heater Leak Repair in Oxford, MS

    Water on the floor next to the heater needs a closer look before anyone panics. A drip from the relief valve or a supply fitting is usually a modest repair. Tank body leaks are different, because a failed inner lining cannot be patched. On a slab floor, a drain pan buys time.

    Pilot Repair in Oxford

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Oxford, MS

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Relief valve, drain valve and supply line drips are cheap fixes. Water from the tank body or bottom seam usually means the lining has failed, and replacement is the only real fix. We protect your floors either way.

    Electric Water Heater Repair in Oxford

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect.     Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Oxford

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. We test each one, clean the burner and pilot assembly and check draft at the vent.
