    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Horn Lake, MS

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Backups add cost. We list each option on a written quote.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Horn Lake, MS

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Horn Lake

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Horn Lake

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Horn Lake

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Horn Lake, MS

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Horn Lake

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Horn Lake, MS

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. They make matching backup units. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.
