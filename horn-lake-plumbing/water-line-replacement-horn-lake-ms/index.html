    PEX Water Line Installation in Horn Lake, MS

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Exposed pipes in crawlspaces, outdoor spigots and well equipment are the first to fail during a rare deep cold snap. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Horn Lake

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Horn Lake, MS

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Horn Lake

    Sometimes the open trench wins. If the old pipe has collapsed or turns hard corners, we dig. Sod goes back on top.
