    Water Heater Installation in Horn Lake

    Sump Pump Installation in Horn Lake, MS

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. The pit goes to proper depth, the primary pump is sized for the inflow, and a battery backup is added on request. Discharge placement matters most. We run the discharge line far enough from the foundation that the water does not cycle straight back into the pit.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Horn Lake, MS

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. An ejector pump in a sealed basin lifts it up to the main drain. We size the pump and basin to the fixtures it serves, vent the basin to code, and put a check valve and a full-port shutoff on the discharge so the pump can be pulled for service. Brands we trust here are Liberty and Zoeller.

    Water Line Installation and Replacement in Horn Lake

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Horn Lake

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector.     Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Horn Lake, MS

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. CSST must be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    PEX Repiping in Horn Lake

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. Wrapping exposed lines and letting faucets drip on the coldest nights prevent most of the damage a sudden freeze can cause. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.
