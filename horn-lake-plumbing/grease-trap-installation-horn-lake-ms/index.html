    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Horn Lake

    Flow rating is the number that matters on a hydromechanical trap, and it has to match the fastest-draining fixture combination in your kitchen. A 50 GPM trap on a line that sees 75 GPM will pass grease every lunch rush. We do the math on paper. Each sink compartment's volume, the drain size and a one or two minute drain time give us the GPM figure the code uses.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Horn Lake

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. The tech walks the kitchen and writes down every fixture that drains to the trap and how fast each one empties. From there we pick a hydromechanical unit or a gravity tank and figure out where it can sit with a straight shot to the building sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Commercial Grease Trap Installation in Horn Lake

    A commercial kitchen with a three-compartment sink, a pre-rinse station and two floor sinks can push 50 gallons a minute of greasy water at the dinner rush. Our sizing starts from your peak flow, the worst ten minutes of the night, rather than a daily average. Undersized traps are the usual failure. The install covers setting the unit, piping the inlet and outlet, and adding the vented flow control the maker specifies.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Horn Lake

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. We confirm the H-20 load rating before the tank ships. Most go in over two to four days. We set the inlet a few inches higher than the outlet and check both with a laser before any dirt goes back in.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Horn Lake, MS

    Concrete or polyethylene, a buried gravity interceptor is a two-compartment tank with tees on the inlet and outlet that reach down below the grease layer. Concrete is heavier and holds up under traffic. Poly is easier to set. With a plastic tank in wet ground we add anti-flotation anchoring, because an empty tank in saturated soil can pop right out of the hole.

    Replacement and Upgrade Services in Horn Lake, MS

    Steel traps from decades back rust through at the baffle seams, and by then the outlet is usually passing grease straight to the sewer. We run a camera down the outlet first to see how much grease got past the old unit. Sometimes the line needs jetting too. If your seats or menu grew since opening, the swap is the time to step up to a bigger GPM rating.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Horn Lake, MS

    Buffets, grocery delis and kitchens doing several hundred covers a night usually end up with an in-ground gravity interceptor of 1,000 gallons or more. The work is excavation and a level gravel bed, then the tank and its inlet and outlet piping. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. We plan the dig and the backfill around what the soil report and the open hole actually show us. Risers bring the lids to grade.
