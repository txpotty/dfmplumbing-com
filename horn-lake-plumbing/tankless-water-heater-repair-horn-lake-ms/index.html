    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Horn Lake

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. We start with the error history in the service menu, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. Venting gets checked too. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Horn Lake

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Horn Lake

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. We check the vent terminal for bird nests and dryer lint, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. Vent runs longer than the manual allows can trigger it too. We measure.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Horn Lake

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. A dead pump or a stuck check valve means you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, look for a thermal bypass valve under the farthest sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Horn Lake

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that comes out of hard water as it heats up, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages. Homes around Horn Lake on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a treatment system. Our tech connects a pump to the isolation valves and runs descaler through the exchanger for close to an hour. Then we flush with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Horn Lake

    Navien condensing units run a second stainless exchanger that pulls heat from the exhaust. That makes condensate, and a plugged trap or a full neutralizer will shut the heater down with a drain or flue error. We clear the trap, check the PVC vent pitch and test the circulation pump on NPE-A models with built-in recirculation. Then we fire it at low and high flow.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Horn Lake, MS

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, so a single burned element leaves the heater behind at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.
