    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Horn Lake, MS

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. A correctly sized pump, or a variable-speed drive, can cut the run time. Not always worth it. We will show you the payback.

    Well Pump Sizing in Horn Lake

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pumps bigger than the well can supply run dry.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Horn Lake

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. If the pump clicks on every time someone runs a faucet for ten seconds, the bladder in the tank has probably failed and the pump is short cycling itself to death. We check the air charge with a gauge. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Horn Lake

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. Both use a small 2 to 20 gallon tank instead of a big one, since the drive keeps the pump running at a matched speed. The soft start is easier on the motor. Expect to pay more up front.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Horn Lake, MS

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Goulds and Franklin parts are on every supply shelf, which helps when a control box fails late on a Saturday afternoon.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Horn Lake

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. Timing matters on a ranch.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Horn Lake

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Where the pump hangs matters.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Horn Lake

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Parts and control boxes are easy to find. We match the motor to the pump end and the wire size to the run length.
