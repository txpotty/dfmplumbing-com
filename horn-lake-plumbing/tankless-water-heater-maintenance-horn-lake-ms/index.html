    Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Horn Lake

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Horn Lake is a proactive measure against costly repairs.     Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Horn Lake

    Proper ventilation is critical for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Horn Lake includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We confirm that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Horn Lake, MS

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in easier service.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Horn Lake

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Flushing in Horn Lake

    Homes around Horn Lake on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a filter. Hard water can lead to significant mineral buildup in tankless water heaters. Our residential flushing service in Horn Lake involves circulating a descaling solution through your unit's heat exchanger to remove calcium and magnesium deposits. This process brings back efficiency, prevents overheating and extends the life of your appliance, so your family gets continuous hot water without interruption.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Horn Lake

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is shielded where it runs outdoors.
