    Foundation Leak Detection in Horn Lake

    Accurate detection is the key step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Water Line Reroute in Horn Lake

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Horn Lake, MS

    Leaks in drain lines under the foundation can be harder to detect because they are not under constant pressure. We use camera inspection and hydrostatic testing to locate cracks and separated joints. Repairs may involve tunneling, cutting the slab or pipe lining. We explain the trade-offs of each method.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Horn Lake

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs requires care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When risk is too high, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Horn Lake

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use low-impact methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these elusive leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, limiting disruption to your home. We work with various pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, ensuring a durable repair for your Horn Lake property.
