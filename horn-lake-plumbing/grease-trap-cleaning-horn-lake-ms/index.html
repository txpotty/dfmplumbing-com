    Why Choose DFM Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Horn Lake, MS?

    Skipped pump-outs usually announce themselves at the floor drain first, with gray water pooling around the grate after the dish machine drains. Then comes the smell. A rancid, sour odor means the grease cap has started to break down and give off hydrogen sulfide. The same gas corrodes concrete, which shortens the life of an in-ground interceptor.

    • System Longevity, Less grease in the line means fewer line jettings and a longer life for the drain pipe.
    • Prevent Backups, Pumping on schedule keeps grease from leaving the trap and hardening in the lateral.
    • Flexible Scheduling, We work before you open or after close, so the kitchen keeps cooking.
