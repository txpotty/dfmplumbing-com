    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Horn Lake

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can crack drain lines and supply pipes. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
