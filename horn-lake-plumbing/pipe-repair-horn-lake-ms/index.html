    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Horn Lake, MS

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way.     PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Horn Lake

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Usually a quick fix.
