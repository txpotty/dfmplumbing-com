    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Vicksburg

    Properties with chronic drain issues benefit from scheduled jetting. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Vicksburg

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Main Line Jetting in Vicksburg, MS

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Vicksburg

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Vicksburg, MS

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Flash flooding can overwhelm storm drains and back up sewer lines, particularly in older neighborhoods. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Vicksburg, MS

    A full sewer backup needs a truck today. First, we open the line. Usually a cable gets the water moving, and then we jet the line to remove what caused the backup, whether that is a grease plug, a root mass or a buildup of wipes caught on a rough joint.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Vicksburg

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain means the main is blocked. Stop using water first. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.
