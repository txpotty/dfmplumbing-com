    Pipe Bursting Services in Vicksburg, MS

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is highly effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a solid solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Vicksburg, MS.

    Underground Sewer Line Repair in Vicksburg, MS

    Buried sewer laterals crack from root intrusion, soil settling and age. We camera the line to find the exact spot, then excavate and replace the damaged section with PVC. Shielded couplings make a watertight connection to the existing pipe. We confirm proper slope and flow before backfilling and tamping the trench.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Vicksburg

    Trenchless pipe repair offers a less invasive alternative to traditional excavation for buried pipe issues.     Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Vicksburg

    Drip lines and sprinkler supply pipes are often shallow and easy to break during landscaping or by roots. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in summer.     Main Water Line Repair in Vicksburg

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house and is under constant pressure. When it leaks, you may notice a spinning meter dial with all fixtures off, low pressure or a wet spot that never dries. We locate the leak, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. If the line is old galvanized or polybutylene, we will recommend a full replacement before more breaks occur.

    Sewer Line Repair in Vicksburg

    Sewer line damage underground can show up as foul odors, slow drains or soggy spots in your yard. We diagnose breaks, blockages or collapses in your main sewer line using advanced camera inspections.     Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Vicksburg

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are usually temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.
