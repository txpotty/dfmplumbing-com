    PEX Pipe Replacement in Vicksburg

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Freezing weather is infrequent in Mississippi, but a hard freeze every few winters can catch homes that were never built for it. It often survives freezing where copper would crack.     Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Vicksburg

    Apartments and condos share risers. Shared lines mean shared problems. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, keeping each unit informed. Organized work.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Vicksburg

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days.     Commercial Pipe Replacement in Vicksburg

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Vicksburg, MS

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.
