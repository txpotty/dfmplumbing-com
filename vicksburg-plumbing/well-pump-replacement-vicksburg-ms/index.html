    Emergency Well Pump Service in Vicksburg, MS

    Lost water at the house? Call (601) 265-9260 and tell us what the pressure gauge reads. The gauge reading, with the pump running or silent, tells us whether to bring a pump or just a control box. We try to restore water the same day.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Vicksburg, MS

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Vicksburg

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. They get by with a much smaller tank because the drive matches pump speed to demand. The soft start is easier on the motor. Expect to pay more up front.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Vicksburg

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. Every pump job includes a tank check.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Vicksburg, MS

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. A correctly sized pump, or a variable-speed drive, can cut the run time. Not always worth it. We will show you the payback.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Vicksburg, MS

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Goulds and Franklin parts are on every supply shelf, which helps when a control box fails late on a Saturday afternoon.
