    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Vicksburg, MS

    Water Heater Inspections in Vicksburg

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Vicksburg

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. Our pre-purchase inspection covers the supply pipe type, the sewer condition on video, the water heater's age and every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Vicksburg, MS

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Catching it early keeps it small. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can crack drain lines and supply pipes.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Vicksburg, MS

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Some need replacing soon. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Vicksburg

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Report in Vicksburg

    Before buying a home in Vicksburg, a plumber's inspection can keep you from inheriting a sewer replacement. Each finding gets a photo, a short description and a repair range, which your agent can put to work in negotiations. Most buyers get it the same day. It fits tight closing schedules.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Vicksburg

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Vicksburg

We test each one directly. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.
