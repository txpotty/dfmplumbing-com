    Sewer Repair Methods for Vicksburg, MS Homes

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Vicksburg, MS

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, which is why we look at the vent whenever the lateral checks out fine. Fixes range from a quick clearing to a new section of stack.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Vicksburg

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Minor sags clog quickly under that load. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and provide inspection video that property managers can hand to owners or insurers. We can work outside business hours.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Vicksburg, MS

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Vicksburg

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit. Older homes in Vicksburg often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Vicksburg, MS

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, so nothing in the yard gets disturbed. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Vicksburg

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Vicksburg, MS

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Vicksburg yards make this a common call.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Vicksburg, MS

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Low, wet ground in many areas also means slow drainage, which matters for sewer lines and septic fields alike. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.
