    Igniter and Flame Rod Repair in Vicksburg, MS

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. An oxidized flame rod gives a weak microamp reading and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Vicksburg, MS

    Rinnai units are common, and scale causes most of their service calls. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and each part gets tested against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Vicksburg

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Resetting the code without a flush only buys a few days, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, run descaler through the exchanger for about an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.

    After-Hours Tankless Repair in Vicksburg, MS

    Some tankless failures will not wait for Monday. A leak at the exchanger or a gas odor at the heater means we come out after hours in Vicksburg. If you smell gas, leave the house first and call the gas utility from outside, then call us, and we can shut the gas cock at the heater and isolate the water until the repair is done.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Vicksburg

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. Bird nests and dryer lint at the terminal come first, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. Vent runs longer than the manual allows can trigger it too. We measure.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Vicksburg, MS

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, yet the checks behind each one are not the same. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Vicksburg

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. We pull the screen, read sensor values in the service menu and compare thermistor resistance to the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Vicksburg

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, since pressure that sags under load leads to flame loss and slugs of cold water. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.
