    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Vicksburg

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Vicksburg

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Vicksburg, MS

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Vicksburg, MS

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Vicksburg, MS

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. We fill the bowl to the overflow, pull the stopper, and watch every nut under the cabinet at full flow with a flashlight and a dry paper towel. A towel shows a weep fast. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Vicksburg, MS

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.
