    Common Faucet Repairs in Vicksburg, MS

    Disc Faucet Repair in Vicksburg

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Vicksburg

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. It happens faster with hard water. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Vicksburg

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Vicksburg

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Vicksburg

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Vicksburg, MS

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Vicksburg, MS

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Homes around Vicksburg on wells or small rural water systems should test their water before choosing a filter. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. Low flow everywhere is a different problem.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Vicksburg, MS

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.
