    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Jackson, MS

    Sewer lines in Jackson, MS can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Jackson

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Jackson, MS

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Jackson, MS

    While the upfront cost of trenchless repair might sometimes seem higher than traditional excavation, it often becomes more cost-effective when factoring in the savings on landscaping, driveway or foundation repair.     Pipe Bursting in Jackson, MS

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.
