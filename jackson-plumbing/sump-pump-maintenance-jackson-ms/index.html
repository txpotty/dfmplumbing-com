    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Jackson, MS

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. Each pump gets tested, along with the alternator and the high-water alarm.     Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Jackson

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. After that, the pump gets a real water test. Low, wet ground in many areas also means slow drainage, which matters for sewer lines and septic fields alike.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Jackson

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. We send a plumber with floats, check valves and spare pumps on the truck. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Jackson

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Float Switch Checks in Jackson, MS

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Next we adjust the on and off levels so each run is longer and the starts are fewer.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Jackson

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Jackson, MS

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Plenty of crawlspaces and deep window wells use a small pump to clear storm runoff. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Freezing weather is infrequent in Mississippi, but a hard freeze every few winters can catch homes that were never built for it.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Jackson, MS

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. The float rod gets checked for bends, and the stops get checked for slipping. Bearings get a listen. Rattling or a high whine at startup usually means the motor is near the end.
