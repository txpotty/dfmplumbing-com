    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Jackson, MS

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Jackson

    Most households in Jackson do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Wiring and testing come last. We listen for a smooth spin and watch the drain clear.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Jackson

    For a common 1/2 or 3/4 HP disposal, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Jackson homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Tight cabinets need planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Jackson, MS

    The old unit usually drops free with a turn of the mount ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. The old disposal leaves with us.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Jackson, MS

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Jackson, MS

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. These units weigh more. They get supported during mounting.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Jackson

    For kitchens that have never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. We install the mount, hang the disposal and connect the discharge tube to a new trap, lowering the drain at the wall if the old stub sits too high for the disposal outlet. An electrician adds power if needed.

    Commercial Disposal Installation in Jackson

    Commercial disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and typically come with a control panel and solenoid valve and need their own circuit. We set the unit on the mounting collar, run the drain to the grease trap where the local rules require it, and wire the controls so the water runs whenever the motor does. Health codes vary. We check the requirement before we order anything.
