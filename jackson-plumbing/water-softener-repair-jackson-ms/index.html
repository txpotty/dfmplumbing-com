    Resin Testing and Replacement in Jackson

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Jackson

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. DFM Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.
