    Descaling Tankless Units in Jackson

    Electric Element Service in Jackson, MS

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. We test with power off. We only replace what failed.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Jackson

    Businesses cannot run without hot water. Restaurants, salons and gyms in Jackson lean on large tanks or banks of tankless units, and a failure during business hours means closing a kitchen or sending customers home. Off-peak visits are available. Big tanks get the full checklist.

    Anode Rod Checks in Jackson

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. It comes out for inspection. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Jackson
