    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilet Installation in Jackson

    Comfort-height toilets put the seat at about 17 to 19 inches, which is easier on knees and backs.     Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Jackson

    Pressure-assisted toilets provide a powerful flush, ideal for commercial settings or homes with specific needs. Their installation differs from gravity-fed models, requiring careful attention to the pressure tank and water supply.     Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Jackson, MS

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is easiest during a remodel when the wall is open.
