    What a Plumbing Checkup Covers in Jackson, MS

    Water Softener Service in Jackson

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. Hardness gets tested after. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Commercial Plumbing Maintenance in Jackson, MS

    Businesses cannot close for a plumbing failure. Restaurants, offices and retail spaces in Jackson have larger water heaters, more restrooms and grease-heavy kitchen lines, all of which wear faster and cost more when they quit during business hours. Scheduled visits prevent that. Off-hours visits are available.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Jackson

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. Sediment drain, anode check, burner or element inspection and a relief valve test cover both tank and tankless. Cold showers are avoidable. We spot the signs early.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Jackson

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Yearly tests are often required, and our certified testers check each valve and file results. Failed devices get rebuilt. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Testing on time avoids fines.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Jackson

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Iron can leave orange stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Flushing takes about an hour. Less sediment means quicker heating and a longer tank life.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Jackson, MS

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Freezing weather is infrequent in Mississippi, but a hard freeze every few winters can catch homes that were never built for it. During maintenance we look for exposed pipes in garages and crawl spaces, hose bibs without vacuum breakers and outdoor lines that need draining, and we fix those before the season that tests them. Pipe sleeves cost little. Burst pipes are not.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Jackson

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Jackson, MS

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. Pipe material matters too. Polybutylene or old galvanized supply lines go on the report with repipe options.
