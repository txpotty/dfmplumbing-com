    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Jackson, MS

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Jackson

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Jackson

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Jackson

    The main from the meter to the house feeds every fixture you own. A leaking old service line shows up as a soggy patch in the yard, a higher water bill and weak pressure everywhere. New PEX goes in its own trench. At the house we add a new full-port ball valve for the main shutoff.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Jackson, MS

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. Cold weather slows it. We let joints set before testing.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Jackson

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Jackson, MS

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Jackson

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Jackson

A shower valve set two inches too low or a vanity stub that misses the cabinet back means opening the wall again after the tile is up, which is expensive and slow. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. Then we pressure test with the walls open.
