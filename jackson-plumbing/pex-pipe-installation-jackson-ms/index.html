    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Jackson, MS

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Jackson, MS

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Jackson

    DFM Plumbing frequently installs Uponor PEX systems, a leading brand known for high-quality, durable PEX-A piping. Uponor's ProPEX expansion fittings create strong, reliable and leak-resistant connections, ideal for the demands of a home's plumbing. We recommend Uponor for its proven track record and longevity, giving Jackson homeowners peace of mind about their plumbing investment.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Jackson

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes made of galvanized steel or polybutylene, a whole-home PEX repipe is an effective solution. We remove the outdated piping and install a complete PEX system, improving water pressure and water quality and eliminating costly leaks. This comprehensive service leaves your entire plumbing system modern, efficient and built to last for decades in your Jackson home.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Jackson, MS

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Jackson

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Iron can leave orange stains on fixtures and laundry, and sulfur odors often trace back to the water heater anode or the well itself. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Jackson

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Jackson can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Jackson

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a great time to replace old supply lines with PEX. We rough in new lines to the correct heights for vanities, showers, tubs and appliances, then pressure test before the walls are closed. Additions and guest suites get correctly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule on track.
