    Foundation, Shower and Block Wall Sealing in Jackson, MS

    Drainage System Integration in Jackson, MS

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. Downspouts get extended at least four feet out.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Jackson

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Jackson

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Jackson, MS

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Jackson, MS

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Jackson

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Jackson

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Jackson take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and meet the waterproofing specs in the building plans for below-grade walls. We keep downtime short.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Jackson, MS

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.
