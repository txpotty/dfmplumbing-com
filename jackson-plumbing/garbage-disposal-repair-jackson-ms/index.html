    Grinding Noise Repair in Jackson

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. After checking the chamber, snugging the mount and pulling debris, we run it empty to hear if the noise is coming from the motor. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring, and we also test air switches and batch-feed lids that use a magnet to start the motor. Most are simple fixes.
