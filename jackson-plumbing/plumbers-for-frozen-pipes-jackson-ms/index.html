    Time matters once a line freezes, because the longer ice sits, the more pressure builds behind it. Open the faucet on that line now. Most frozen lines flow again within an hour of arrival, and every one gets a pressure test.

    Frozen Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Jackson, MS

    Thawing an accessible frozen pipe usually costs $150 to $400. Repairing a split costs more. Burst repairs run $300 to $1,000 depending on access, and prevention work usually costs a few hundred.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Jackson

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Those get heat cable. Old-style constant-wattage heat tape can overheat and is a known fire risk, so we install self-regulating cable listed for the pipe material and replace worn or brittle heat tape we find.

    Preventive Pipe Insulation in Jackson

    Foam pipe insulation costs little and prevents most freezes in attics and crawlspaces. Fit matters. We size the sleeve to the pipe, tape every seam and miter the corners so there are no gaps at elbows and tees, since a single bare fitting is often exactly where a line freezes first.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Jackson

    Drain lines can freeze too, usually where a line runs through an unheated crawlspace or an exterior wall. A frozen drain backs up. We thaw drains with a steam or hot-water jetter fed through the cleanout, then check the slope, because a drain that holds water in a sag is the kind that freezes again next time.

    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Jackson, MS

    Finding the frozen spot is the hard part. A thermal camera speeds it up. By checking which fixtures work and which do not, then scanning walls and ceilings with the camera, we can usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe and thaw it without cutting into the wall.
