    Most jurisdictions in Mississippi require a permit for water heater replacement, especially when gas piping or venting changes. We handle permits where they are required.

    Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include repeated error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water.     DIY tankless water heater replacement is generally not recommended due to the complexity of gas lines, high-voltage electricity, water connections and venting requirements.     DFM Plumbing installs and replaces all major trusted brands of tankless water heaters.     Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail far sooner. Annual flushing makes the biggest difference.
