    Many older homes in Jackson, MS still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup.     Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Jackson, MS

    Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral accumulation, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Jackson, MS

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. DFM Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Jackson, MS, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup.
