    Copper Service Line Installation in Jackson, MS

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Jackson

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. The zinc coating wears off and the steel rusts from the inside. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, and that shows up as weak pressure and orange water, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Jackson

    Sometimes the open trench wins. A collapsed line, a route with sharp bends, or solid rock can rule out trenchless. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, lay the new pipe on a sand bed, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in Jackson

    Strip centers, churches and small apartment buildings each come with their own wrinkles. A single meter may feed six or eight units. We map where each branch goes before cutting anything, coordinate with the utility for any work at the meter, and handle the permit, backflow test and inspection so the owner gets one point of contact. Paperwork included.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Jackson, MS

    Commercial services run bigger, often 1-1/2 to 2 inches, and almost always have a backflow assembly that needs testing after any work. Shutdowns get scheduled for when you are closed. For a restaurant or an office building that might mean an early morning start, temporary water to the restrooms, and a call to the utility about their side of the meter. Tenants get notice.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Jackson

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Burst Main Line Response in Jackson, MS

    Burst lines do damage fast. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Jackson

    We start at the meter. If the little dial on the meter is spinning with every fixture off, water is leaving somewhere. From there we listen along the line with an acoustic ground mic, rule out a running toilet or water softener, and sometimes push a camera into the line when we suspect root damage or a crushed section, so the hole goes in the right spot.
