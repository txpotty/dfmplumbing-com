    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Hattiesburg, MS

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Hattiesburg, MS

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. Water heaters in hot garages and attics wear faster, so their condition is worth checking during any plumbing visit. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier and healthier environment in your Hattiesburg, MS basement.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Hattiesburg

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can crack drain lines and supply pipes. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Hattiesburg, MS

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades prevent costly drywall repairs.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Hattiesburg, MS

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.
