    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Hattiesburg

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Hattiesburg yard.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Hattiesburg

    Older Hattiesburg homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Hattiesburg

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and stands up to roots.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Hattiesburg

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They hold up in tough conditions.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Hattiesburg, MS

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. DFM Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Hattiesburg, MS

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Restoration changes that. Open trenching under a drive or garden adds concrete, plant and sprinkler replacement to the bill, which trenchless mostly avoids. We price both side by side.
