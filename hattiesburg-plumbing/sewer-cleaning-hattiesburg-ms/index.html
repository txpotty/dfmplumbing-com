    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Hattiesburg

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Hattiesburg, MS

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Older homes in Mississippi often have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains, both of which corrode and restrict flow with age. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Hattiesburg

    The main carries everything out of the house. A blockage there stops every drain. We run heavy sectional cable with a cutter sized for 3 or 4 inch pipe, working from the cleanout toward the street until the head turns freely and water runs at full speed, which is common work in the older homes around Hattiesburg.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Hattiesburg, MS

    Commercial lines take a beating. Kitchens put grease into the line daily. Offices and retail buildings deal more with paper towels and foreign objects flushed in public restrooms, which snag on joints and catch everything after them, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Hattiesburg

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. Leaky joints feed them all year. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out, then follow with a jetter if fine root hairs are left behind, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Hattiesburg, MS

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. A crack stays a crack. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Hattiesburg, MS

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Hattiesburg

    Household sewers clog slowly. Grease, hair and wipes build up over months. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.
