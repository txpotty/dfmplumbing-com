    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Hattiesburg, MS

    New Sump Pit Installation in Hattiesburg

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Hattiesburg, MS

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before the rainy season is a smart move.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Hattiesburg

    Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement. We install exterior sump basins with pumps that move storm water to a safe discharge point. These systems are helpful for lots that slope toward the house. We route discharge lines so water drains away from the foundation and neighbors. This helps prevent siding damage and foundation problems.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, well suited for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    A well-maintained sump pump usually lasts about 7 to 10 years. However, its lifespan can vary based on usage frequency, water quality and pump quality. Regular inspections by a professional can help determine if your sump pump in Hattiesburg is nearing the end of its service life.

    A simple replacement often does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.
