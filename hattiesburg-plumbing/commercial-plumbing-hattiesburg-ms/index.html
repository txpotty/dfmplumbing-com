    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Hattiesburg

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can shift drain lines and supply pipes. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. We price both so you can compare.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Hattiesburg

Sewage backups get the same response. After clearing the line we run a camera so you know whether it is a one-time clog or damaged pipe.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Hattiesburg

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Passing devices get tagged. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.
