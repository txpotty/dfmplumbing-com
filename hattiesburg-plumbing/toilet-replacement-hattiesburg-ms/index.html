    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Hattiesburg, MS

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. Getting one up a staircase takes two people, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Hattiesburg

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Hattiesburg

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Hattiesburg, MS

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Hattiesburg, MS

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Hattiesburg, MS

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. You get repair and replacement prices next to each other. The decision is yours, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Hattiesburg

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then we level it. We caulk the base except for a small gap at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Hattiesburg

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. You can pick battery or hardwired sensors. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.
