    Compression Faucet Repair in Hattiesburg

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Hattiesburg, MS

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Hattiesburg

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Exposed pipes in crawlspaces, outdoor spigots and well equipment are the most vulnerable during a rare deep cold snap. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Hattiesburg, MS

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Hattiesburg, MS

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. Commercial cartridges are on the truck. Metering faucets get timed so they run long enough to rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Hattiesburg, MS

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. Ask us to check. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard use their own cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Hattiesburg

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.
