    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Hattiesburg

    The cost for gas water heater replacement in Hattiesburg, MS typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,000, including the unit and installation. This can vary based on the model, capacity and any necessary changes to gas lines or venting. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    Most cities and counties in Mississippi require a permit for water heater replacement because it involves gas and venting. We take care of the permit when one is required.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to more complete combustion.

    New gas water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. DFM Plumbing also provides a warranty on our installation workmanship, giving you double peace of mind.

    Replacing a gas water heater involves working with gas lines, water connections and venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional like DFM Plumbing for safety and code compliance.

    Most standard gas water heater replacements in Hattiesburg can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a different type of unit or significant plumbing changes, may take longer.

    Most gas tanks last 8 to 12 years. Sediment from hard water and extreme garage temperatures can cut that. Annual flushing and anode checks help them last for more years.

    Water pooling at the base, rusty hot water, rumbling or popping sounds and a tank over 10 years old are common signs. A leak from the tank itself is not repairable.
