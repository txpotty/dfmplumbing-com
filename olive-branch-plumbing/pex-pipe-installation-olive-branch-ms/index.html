    What Goes Into a PEX Install in Olive Branch, MS

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Olive Branch

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Olive Branch

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. If the shower valve sits two inches low or the vanity stubs miss the cabinet, the wall gets reopened after tile, and that costs time and money. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. The test happens before drywall goes up.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Olive Branch

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. A whole-home repipe will. Every line from the main valve to each angle stop gets replaced, and the system is tested before anything gets closed up.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Olive Branch, MS

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Olive Branch

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Olive Branch, MS

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Olive Branch

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, though not the same pipe you drink from. Heating work calls for oxygen-barrier PEX, coated so oxygen cannot seep through the pipe wall and corrode the boiler, the circulator and the iron parts. Loops have length limits. Run them too long and the last stretch never warms up.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Olive Branch, MS

    A single bad section does not always mean a repipe. For one split or pitted stretch of copper, we cut it out, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and usually restore water within a couple of hours. That works for one leak. By the third leak, though, we will talk to you honestly about whether the rest of that copper is close behind.
