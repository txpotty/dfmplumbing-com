    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Olive Branch

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in often run $800 to $2,500.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light breaks down it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can break down the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is strongly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are essential for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has far fewer joints. Copper is very durable but prone to pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    We install quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other trusted brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage adds to its long-term reliability, making it a smart investment for your Olive Branch home.

    Whole-home repipes typically require a plumbing permit and inspection. We handle the permit process as part of the project.
