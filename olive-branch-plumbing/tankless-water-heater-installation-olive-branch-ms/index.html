    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Olive Branch

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow.     Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Olive Branch, MS

    Businesses in Olive Branch, MS with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Olive Branch, MS

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Much of the water in Mississippi comes from underground aquifers and is fairly soft, though some supplies carry iron or a sulfur smell. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Olive Branch

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Navien vents in PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Olive Branch

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. That narrows the model list fast.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Olive Branch, MS

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Olive Branch, MS homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. We check the panel first.
