    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is often used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.

    The duration of drain repairs depends on the complexity of the problem. Simple clog removals might take an hour or two, while more extensive repairs like replacing a section of a main sewer line could take a full day or more. We'll give you an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    Drain cleaning removes a blockage from an otherwise sound pipe. Drain repair fixes the pipe itself, such as a crack, bad slope, broken fitting or missing vent. Many recurring clogs need repair, not more cleaning.

    The length of drain repairs depends entirely on the issue. A simple clog can often be cleared in an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of a main sewer line, might take a full day or even several days, especially if permits or major digging are involved.
