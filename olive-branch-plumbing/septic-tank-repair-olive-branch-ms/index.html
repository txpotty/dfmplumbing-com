    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Olive Branch, MS

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Olive Branch

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. Bad lids get replaced, and risers bring the opening up to grade. Future pumping gets cheaper. Each lid we install gets screws or a lock so a child cannot lift it.

    Leach Field Restoration in Olive Branch, MS

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A box that has settled or cracked can push all the effluent into a single trench. Leveling or replacing the box often brings the field back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Olive Branch, MS

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Expansive clay soils are common in Mississippi, swelling with rain and shrinking in dry spells, which puts stress on foundations and buried pipes. Roots and settling ground split joints and crush the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, set on sand bedding with a quarter inch per foot of fall. Short sections take an afternoon.

    Drain Field Repair in Olive Branch, MS

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload caused by a leaking fixture are the other usual causes. Testing always comes first. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Olive Branch

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. An alarm going off means the water rose past the high-level float. Our tech meters the pump and tests each float before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Olive Branch

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. A jetter goes into each lateral from the D-box or an end cleanout, and what washes back shows how plugged it was. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

Grease interceptors and lift stations are part of it. Downtime costs you, so we schedule around open hours.
