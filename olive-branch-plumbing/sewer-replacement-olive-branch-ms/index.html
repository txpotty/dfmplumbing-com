    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Olive Branch, MS

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Olive Branch, MS

    Trenching lets us fix grade. Correcting slope is where it shines. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, so we dig and reset the pipe on a proper bed at a proper fall. Bellies disappear.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Olive Branch

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. The inside rusts and scales until the bottom of the pipe thins out and cracks. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Olive Branch

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and use pipe and fittings listed for underground drainage. Paperwork stays on file.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Olive Branch, MS

    Original sewer pipe in older homes is usually clay, cast iron or Orangeburg. Clay joints open up and let roots in, cast iron scales and cracks from the inside, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid 1900s, squashes into an oval over time. Replacement means PVC or HDPE.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Olive Branch

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. We dig with a mini excavator and hand dig near utility marks. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, confirmed with a level on every length. Then the camera.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Olive Branch, MS

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. One sits at the house, the other at the far end of the run. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, so the new line takes the same path without a trench. Driveways stay put.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Olive Branch, MS

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. CIPP lining coats the inside of the existing pipe instead. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, since it puts a new pipe in place instead of reinforcing the old, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Olive Branch

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. Restaurants usually have a grease interceptor in the line too. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and work with the utility on the main connection. Weekend starts are common.

    Our crews work on sewer lines across Olive Branch every week. Neighbors usually have the same pipe material, since streets were built together.
