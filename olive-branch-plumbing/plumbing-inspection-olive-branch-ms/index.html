    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Olive Branch

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Mississippi houses. That list starts with old supply pipe, worn angle stops, aging water heaters, slow drains and sewer lines with roots or sags. Each gets tested, not guessed at. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Olive Branch

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. Our plumber looks at all exposed pipe, runs every faucet and toilet and tests the heater, the main valve and the fixture stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Olive Branch, MS

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Olive Branch, MS

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Report in Olive Branch

    Before buying a home in Olive Branch, a plumber's inspection can keep you from inheriting a sewer replacement. Our report lists every finding with a photo, a plain description and a repair range, so your agent can use it in negotiations right away. Most buyers get it the same day. That timing fits most option periods.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Olive Branch

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. Expansion tanks, drain pans and softeners get a look as well, and a quick hardness test tells us whether the softener is actually doing its job or just using salt.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Olive Branch, MS

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Some need replacing soon. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Olive Branch, MS

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Catching it early keeps it small. Slab and crawlspace foundations are typical, and soil movement under a slab can crack drain lines and supply pipes.
