    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Olive Branch, MS

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. DFM Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Olive Branch, MS.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Olive Branch

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Olive Branch.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Olive Branch

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
