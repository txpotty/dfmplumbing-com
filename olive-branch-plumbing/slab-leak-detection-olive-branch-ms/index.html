    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Olive Branch

    Plastic pipe muffles leak noise. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, even through grout lines and small cracks.     Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Olive Branch

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, listening for the spot where the sound peaks and then checking it again from a different angle, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.
