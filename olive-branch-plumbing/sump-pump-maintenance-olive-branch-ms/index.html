    Battery Backup System Testing in Olive Branch, MS

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Olive Branch

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. We match the float and seals to the model number on the label. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.

    Wayne Sump Pump Service in Olive Branch, MS

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Olive Branch, MS

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. They need checking more often than anything in a house. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Olive Branch

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Olive Branch

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. The property manager gets a copy of every log.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Olive Branch, MS

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. Each pump gets tested, along with the alternator and the high-water alarm. You get a written record of each reading, which comes in handy for insurance renewals and inspections. Quarterly visits are common.
