    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Olive Branch

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer houses and manufactured homes typically rely on PEX or CPVC supply lines with PVC drains. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. We price each one for you.

    Main Water Line Repair in Olive Branch

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth.
