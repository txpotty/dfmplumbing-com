    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Olive Branch

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Low, wet ground in many areas also means slow drainage, which matters for sewer lines and septic fields alike. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Olive Branch

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Olive Branch, MS

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. DFM Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Olive Branch, MS, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also significantly dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Copper Repiping in Olive Branch, MS

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Olive Branch

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more complex. We coordinate with HOAs, schedule water shutoffs and protect neighboring units. For property managers we offer building-wide repipes done in phases to keep residents in place. Each unit gets clear communication and daily cleanup.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Olive Branch, MS

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Olive Branch, MS has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent major water damage. DFM Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a reliable new system.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Olive Branch

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.
