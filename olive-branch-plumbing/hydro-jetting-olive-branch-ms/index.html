    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Olive Branch

    The cost of hydro jetting in Olive Branch, MS usually ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access. More complex jobs, like main sewer line descaling or extensive root removal, may be on the higher end. We provide a clear quote after an initial inspection.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters along with rotating and root-cutting nozzles from well-known manufacturers. Color camera systems record the line before and after.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why DFM Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to assess your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or caved in.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection reveals damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often leaves behind.

    Key signs you might need hydro jetting include regularly recurring clogs in the same drain, multiple slow drains throughout your home, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or gurgling sounds from toilets or drains. These often indicate a significant buildup or blockage that needs more than just a snake.

    A thorough jetting often keeps a line clear for a year or more, depending on use and roots. Lines with heavy root intrusion may require maintenance or a repair.
