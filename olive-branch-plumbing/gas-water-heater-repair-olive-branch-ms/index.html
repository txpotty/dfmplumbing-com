    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Olive Branch

    Most gas water heater repairs cost $150 to $700. Thermocouple and pilot work is at the low end, while gas control valves and power vent components are at the high end. We quote before starting.

    Most repairs are completed the same day in one to two hours. Special order gas valves may take one to three business days.

    Following the label instructions to relight a pilot is usually safe. If the pilot will not stay lit, or you smell gas, stop and call a licensed plumber.

    In most cases, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit.     Replacing a part like a thermocouple or gas valve typically does not. Replacing the water heater or altering gas piping or venting often requires a permit, which we handle.
