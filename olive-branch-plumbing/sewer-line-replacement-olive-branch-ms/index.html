    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Olive Branch, MS

    Two holes instead of one long trench. Pipe bursting drags a cone-shaped head through the old line, cracking it outward while new fused HDPE follows right behind. Lining is the other trenchless route. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Olive Branch

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Olive Branch

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. The bottom of a cast iron pipe goes first, and the camera will show a trench rusted through even when the top looks fine. Rough scale catches everything. Outside the foundation we replace it with PVC or HDPE. For cast iron under a slab, we talk through lining versus rerouting.

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency.     Most of our sewer work is single-family homes. Low, wet ground in many areas also means poor drainage, which matters for sewer lines and septic fields alike.
